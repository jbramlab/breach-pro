Coastline · 1F · Site 3 of 4

Kitchen / Service Entrance

Best guess The two bomb rooms on this plan are our best guess from the blueprint. Check it in a custom game before you rely on it.

You plus four randoms
AttackSolo1FDraft · Y11S3

Bring your own breach or your own anti-denial.

The plan

  1. Ace or Maverick opens the Service Entrance wall to Toilets even with no help.
  2. Drone Kitchen Hallway for your randoms and call what you see.

Operators · first pick is you

  • ACAce
  • Maverick
  • Thatcher
  • Kali

Common mistake

Breaching without droning, so the denier just stays alive.

AttackDuo1FDraft · Y11S3

Breacher plus support on the wall.

The plan

  1. Thatcher drones the wall, then EMPs the denier.
  2. Thermite opens the Service Entrance wall to Toilets and you both trade into Service Entrance.

Operators · first 2 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • Hibana
  • Kali

Common mistake

Support forgetting to drone again after the EMP.

AttackTrio1FDraft · Y11S3

Two on the wall, one pushing Kitchen Hallway.

The plan

  1. Ash pushes Kitchen Hallway to pull defenders off the wall.
  2. Open the Service Entrance wall to Toilets while they are looking at Ash.

Operators · first 3 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • ASAsh

Common mistake

Entry going in before the wall is open and dying alone.

Attack4-stack1FDraft · Y11S3

Wall team, inside push and a flank watch.

The plan

  1. Nomad holds Courtyard so nobody hits your backs.
  2. Ace and Kali open the Service Entrance wall to Toilets as Ash pushes Kitchen Hallway.
  3. Plant in Service Entrance with the hole covering you.

Operators · first 4 are your squad

  • ACAce
  • KAKali
  • ASAsh
  • NONomad

Common mistake

Everyone on one side with nobody watching the flank.

Attack5-stack1FDraft · Y11S3

Full execute: wall, two pushes and a flank watch.

The plan

  1. 0:00 to 1:00: drone in and take Kitchen Hallway.
  2. Thatcher EMPs and Thermite opens the Service Entrance wall to Toilets at about 1:30.
  3. Ash and Ying hit Kitchen Hallway and Blue Stairs together. Nomad holds Courtyard.

Operators · first 5 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • ASAsh
  • YIYing
  • NONomad

Common mistake

Opening the wall too early so defenders have time to reset.

DefenceSolo1FDraft · Y11S3

Deny the Service Entrance wall to Toilets yourself and play from Kitchen.

The plan

  1. Put your denial on the Service Entrance wall to Toilets so it survives even if your randoms do nothing.
  2. Reinforce the Service Entrance wall to Toilets before anything else.
  3. Call "Kitchen Hallway" the moment you see a drone there.

Operators · first pick is you

  • KDKaid
  • Mute
  • Bandit
  • Mira

Common mistake

Roaming as a solo. Randoms rarely hold site while you are away.

DefenceDuo1FDraft · Y11S3

One anchor per room, one of you on cams.

The plan

  1. Kaid holds Service Entrance and keeps the Service Entrance wall to Toilets electrified.
  2. Valkyrie cams cover Kitchen Hallway and Blue Stairs so you both see pushes early.
  3. Swap between Kitchen and Service Entrance when one side gets hit, and call it before you move.

Operators · first 2 are your squad

  • KDKaid
  • VAValkyrie
  • Bandit
  • Echo

Common mistake

Both of you watching the same doorway while the other room falls.

AttackOperator setup1F

Where each attacker goes on Kitchen / Service Entrance

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Toilets

    Breach the Service Entrance wall to Toilets from Toilets. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Toilets

    Backup breach on the Service Entrance wall to Toilets. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Toilets

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Service Entrance wall to Toilets. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Toilets

    EMP the Service Entrance wall to Toilets right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Kitchen Hallway

    Lance the denial on the Service Entrance wall to Toilets from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Kitchen.

  6. AS

    AshEntry · Kitchen Hallway

    Take Kitchen Hallway first and clear the doorway into Kitchen for the plant.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Blue Stairs

    Drive your shock drone into Kitchen and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Courtyard

    Airjabs at Courtyard so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup1F

Where each defender goes on Kitchen / Service Entrance

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Service Entrance

    Claw the Service Entrance wall to Toilets from inside Service Entrance so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Kitchen / Kitchen Hallway wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Service Entrance

    Sit on the Service Entrance wall to Toilets with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Kitchen

    Jammer by the Kitchen Hallway door into Kitchen. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Kitchen

    Mirror the wall between Kitchen and Service Entrance, so you can watch the plant in Service Entrance from Kitchen without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Kitchen

    ADS on the Blue Stairs side of Kitchen. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Kitchen Hallway

    Cams in Kitchen Hallway and Blue Stairs, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Kitchen

    Anchor Kitchen. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. VI

    VigilRoam · Courtyard

    Roam Courtyard and the rooms around it. Take one fight, then fall back to Kitchen by 1:00.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Service Entrance wall to Toilets from Toilets.
  • Take Kitchen Hallway early and drone the site.
  • Clear Courtyard so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Service Entrance wall to Toilets stays denied, push Kitchen Hallway and Blue Stairs together with smokes and plant in Kitchen.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Service Entrance wall to Toilets first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Kitchen / Kitchen Hallway wall next.
  • Watch Kitchen Hallway and Blue Stairs. That is where the inside push comes from.

If it goes wrong

If the Service Entrance wall to Toilets opens early, fall back into Kitchen and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
