AttackOperator setup1F

Where each attacker goes on Storage / Control Room

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Delivery

    Breach the Storage wall to Delivery from Delivery. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Delivery

    Backup breach on the Storage wall to Delivery. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Delivery

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Storage wall to Delivery. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Delivery

    EMP the Storage wall to Delivery right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Storage Hallway

    Lance the denial on the Storage wall to Delivery from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Control Room.

  6. BU

    BuckVertical · 2F above

    Play Command Center above Control Room. Open small holes over the default anchor spots in Control Room.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Warehouse Entrance

    Drive your shock drone into Control Room and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Workshop Stairs

    Airjabs at Workshop Stairs so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup1F

Where each defender goes on Storage / Control Room

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Storage

    Claw the Storage wall to Delivery from inside Storage so the whole wall is live.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Storage

    Sit on the Storage wall to Delivery with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Storage

    Jammer by the Storage Hallway door into Storage. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Control Room

    Mirror the wall between Control Room and Storage, so you can watch the plant in Storage from Control Room without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Storage

    ADS on the Warehouse Entrance side of Storage. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Storage Hallway

    Cams in Storage Hallway and Warehouse Entrance, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Control Room

    Anchor Control Room. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. PU

    PulseVertical hunt · 2F above

    Go up to Command Center above Control Room early. Pulse attackers setting up vertical and pick them off before they open the floor.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Storage wall to Delivery from Delivery.
  • Play vertical from Command Center above Control Room to pin the anchors.
  • Clear Workshop Stairs so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Storage wall to Delivery stays denied, push Storage Hallway and Warehouse Entrance together with smokes and plant in Control Room.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Storage wall to Delivery first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Keep the way between Storage and Control Room open so anchors can rotate.
  • Watch Command Center above Control Room. Attackers will play vertical from there.

If it goes wrong

If the Storage wall to Delivery opens early, fall back into Control Room and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
