Dormitory / Games Room

Best guess The two bomb rooms on this plan are our best guess from the blueprint. Check it in a custom game before you rely on it.

You plus four randoms
AttackSolo2FDraft · Y11S3

Bring your own breach or your own anti-denial.

The plan

  1. Ace or Maverick opens the Dormitory wall to Side room even with no help.
  2. Drone Main Stairs for your randoms and call what you see.

Operators · first pick is you

  • ACAce
  • Maverick
  • Thatcher
  • Kali

Common mistake

Breaching without droning, so the denier just stays alive.

Breacher plus support on the wall.

  • THThatcher

  • Kali

DefenceSolo2FDraft · Y11S3

  3. Call "Main Stairs" the moment you see a drone there.

  • Bandit
  • Mira

AttackOperator setup2F

Where each attacker goes on Dormitory / Games Room

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Side room

    Breach the Dormitory wall to Side room from Side room. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Side room

    Backup breach on the Dormitory wall to Side room. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Side room

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Dormitory wall to Side room. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Side room

    EMP the Dormitory wall to Side room right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Main Stairs

    Lance the denial on the Dormitory wall to Side room from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Games Room.

  6. AS

    AshEntry · Main Stairs

    Take Main Stairs first and clear the doorway into Dormitory for the plant.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Dormitory Hallway

    Drive your shock drone into Games Room and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Hammam Roof

    Airjabs at Hammam Roof so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup2F

Where each defender goes on Dormitory / Games Room

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Dormitory

    Claw the Dormitory wall to Side room from inside Dormitory so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Side room / Gaming Hallway wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Dormitory

    Sit on the Dormitory wall to Side room with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Dormitory

    Jammer by the Main Stairs door into Dormitory. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Games Room

    Mirror the wall between Games Room and Dormitory, so you can watch the plant in Dormitory from Games Room without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Dormitory

    ADS on the Dormitory Hallway side of Dormitory. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Main Stairs

    Cams in Main Stairs and Dormitory Hallway, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Games Room

    Anchor Games Room. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. VI

    VigilRoam · Hammam Roof

    Roam Hammam Roof and the rooms around it. Take one fight, then fall back to Games Room by 1:00.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Dormitory wall to Side room from Side room.
  • Take Main Stairs early and drone the site.
  • Clear Hammam Roof so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Dormitory wall to Side room stays denied, push Main Stairs and Dormitory Hallway together with smokes and plant in Games Room.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Dormitory wall to Side room first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Side room / Gaming Hallway wall next.
  • Watch Main Stairs and Dormitory Hallway. That is where the inside push comes from.

If it goes wrong

If the Dormitory wall to Side room opens early, fall back into Games Room and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
