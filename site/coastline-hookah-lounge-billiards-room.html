Coastline · 2F · Site 1 of 4

Hookah Lounge / Billiards Room

Best guess The two bomb rooms on this plan are our best guess from the blueprint. Check it in a custom game before you rely on it.

You plus four randoms
AttackSolo2FDraft · Y11S3

Bring your own breach or your own anti-denial.

The plan

  1. Ace or Maverick opens the Hookah Lounge wall to Hookah Hallway even with no help.
  2. Drone Blue Stairs for your randoms and call what you see.

Operators · first pick is you

  • ACAce
  • Maverick
  • Thatcher
  • Kali

Common mistake

Breaching without droning, so the denier just stays alive.

AttackDuo2FDraft · Y11S3

Breacher plus support on the wall.

The plan

  1. Thatcher drones the wall, then EMPs the denier.
  2. Thermite opens the Hookah Lounge wall to Hookah Hallway and you both trade into Hookah Lounge.

Operators · first 2 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • Hibana
  • Kali

Common mistake

Support forgetting to drone again after the EMP.

AttackTrio2FDraft · Y11S3

Two on the wall, one pushing Blue Stairs.

The plan

  1. Ash pushes Blue Stairs to pull defenders off the wall.
  2. Open the Hookah Lounge wall to Hookah Hallway while they are looking at Ash.

Operators · first 3 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • ASAsh

Common mistake

Entry going in before the wall is open and dying alone.

Attack4-stack2FDraft · Y11S3

Wall team, inside push and a flank watch.

The plan

  1. Nomad holds Theater so nobody hits your backs.
  2. Ace and Kali open the Hookah Lounge wall to Hookah Hallway as Ash pushes Blue Stairs.
  3. Plant in Hookah Lounge with the hole covering you.

Operators · first 4 are your squad

  • ACAce
  • KAKali
  • ASAsh
  • NONomad

Common mistake

Everyone on one side with nobody watching the flank.

Attack5-stack2FDraft · Y11S3

Full execute: wall, two pushes and a flank watch.

The plan

  1. 0:00 to 1:00: drone in and take Blue Stairs.
  2. Thatcher EMPs and Thermite opens the Hookah Lounge wall to Hookah Hallway at about 1:30.
  3. Ash and Ying hit Blue Stairs and Hookah Balcony together. Nomad holds Theater.

Operators · first 5 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • ASAsh
  • YIYing
  • NONomad

Common mistake

Opening the wall too early so defenders have time to reset.

DefenceSolo2FDraft · Y11S3

Deny the Hookah Lounge wall to Hookah Hallway yourself and play from Billiards Room.

The plan

  1. Put your denial on the Hookah Lounge wall to Hookah Hallway so it survives even if your randoms do nothing.
  2. Reinforce the Hookah Lounge wall to Hookah Hallway before anything else.
  3. Call "Blue Stairs" the moment you see a drone there.

Operators · first pick is you

  • KDKaid
  • Mute
  • Bandit
  • Mira

Common mistake

Roaming as a solo. Randoms rarely hold site while you are away.

DefenceDuo2FDraft · Y11S3

One anchor per room, one of you on cams.

The plan

  1. Kaid holds Hookah Lounge and keeps the Hookah Lounge wall to Hookah Hallway electrified.
  2. Valkyrie cams cover Blue Stairs and Hookah Balcony so you both see pushes early.
  3. Swap between Hookah Lounge and Billiards Room when one side gets hit, and call it before you move.

Operators · first 2 are your squad

  • KDKaid
  • VAValkyrie
  • Bandit
  • Echo

Common mistake

Both of you watching the same doorway while the other room falls.

AttackOperator setup2F

Where each attacker goes on Hookah Lounge / Billiards Room

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Hookah Hallway

    Breach the Hookah Lounge wall to Hookah Hallway from Hookah Hallway. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Hookah Hallway

    Backup breach on the Hookah Lounge wall to Hookah Hallway. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Hookah Hallway

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Hookah Lounge wall to Hookah Hallway. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Hookah Hallway

    EMP the Hookah Lounge wall to Hookah Hallway right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Blue Stairs

    Lance the denial on the Hookah Lounge wall to Hookah Hallway from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Billiards Room.

  6. AS

    AshEntry · Blue Stairs

    Take Blue Stairs first and clear the doorway into Hookah Lounge for the plant.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Hookah Balcony

    Drive your shock drone into Billiards Room and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Theater

    Airjabs at Theater so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup2F

Where each defender goes on Hookah Lounge / Billiards Room

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Hookah Lounge

    Claw the Hookah Lounge wall to Hookah Hallway from inside Hookah Lounge so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the VIP / Hookah Hallway wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Hookah Lounge

    Sit on the Hookah Lounge wall to Hookah Hallway with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Hookah Lounge

    Jammer by the Blue Stairs door into Hookah Lounge. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Billiards Room

    Mirror the wall between Billiards Room and Hookah Lounge, so you can watch the plant in Hookah Lounge from Billiards Room without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Billiards Room

    ADS on the Hookah Balcony side of Billiards Room. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Blue Stairs

    Cams in Blue Stairs and Hookah Balcony, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Billiards Room

    Anchor Billiards Room. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. VI

    VigilRoam · Theater

    Roam Theater and the rooms around it. Take one fight, then fall back to Billiards Room by 1:00.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Hookah Lounge wall to Hookah Hallway from Hookah Hallway.
  • Take Blue Stairs early and drone the site.
  • Clear Theater so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Hookah Lounge wall to Hookah Hallway stays denied, push Blue Stairs and Hookah Balcony together with smokes and plant in Billiards Room.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Hookah Lounge wall to Hookah Hallway first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the VIP / Hookah Hallway wall next.
  • Watch Blue Stairs and Hookah Balcony. That is where the inside push comes from.

If it goes wrong

If the Hookah Lounge wall to Hookah Hallway opens early, fall back into Billiards Room and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
