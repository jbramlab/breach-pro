Kafe Dostoyevsky · 3F · Site 1 of 4

Cocktail Lounge / Bar

Best guess The two bomb rooms on this plan are our best guess from the blueprint. Check it in a custom game before you rely on it.

You plus four randoms
AttackSolo3FDraft · Y11S3

Bring your own breach or your own anti-denial.

The plan

  1. Ace or Maverick opens the Cocktail Lounge wall to Cocktail Balcony even with no help.
  2. Drone White Stairs for your randoms and call what you see.

Operators · first pick is you

  • ACAce
  • Maverick
  • Thatcher
  • Kali

Common mistake

Breaching without droning, so the denier just stays alive.

AttackDuo3FDraft · Y11S3

Breacher plus support on the wall.

The plan

  1. Thatcher drones the wall, then EMPs the denier.
  2. Thermite opens the Cocktail Lounge wall to Cocktail Balcony and you both trade into Cocktail Lounge.

Operators · first 2 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • Hibana
  • Kali

Common mistake

Support forgetting to drone again after the EMP.

AttackTrio3FDraft · Y11S3

Two on the wall, one pushing White Stairs.

The plan

  1. Ash pushes White Stairs to pull defenders off the wall.
  2. Open the Cocktail Lounge wall to Cocktail Balcony while they are looking at Ash.

Operators · first 3 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • ASAsh

Common mistake

Entry going in before the wall is open and dying alone.

Attack4-stack3FDraft · Y11S3

Wall team, inside push and a flank watch.

The plan

  1. Nomad holds White Corridor so nobody hits your backs.
  2. Ace and Kali open the Cocktail Lounge wall to Cocktail Balcony as Ash pushes White Stairs.
  3. Plant in Cocktail Lounge with the hole covering you.

Operators · first 4 are your squad

  • ACAce
  • KAKali
  • ASAsh
  • NONomad

Common mistake

Everyone on one side with nobody watching the flank.

Attack5-stack3FDraft · Y11S3

Full execute: wall, two pushes and a flank watch.

The plan

  1. 0:00 to 1:00: drone in and take White Stairs.
  2. Thatcher EMPs and Thermite opens the Cocktail Lounge wall to Cocktail Balcony at about 1:30.
  3. Ash and Ying hit White Stairs and Cocktail Entrance together. Nomad holds White Corridor.

Operators · first 5 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • ASAsh
  • YIYing
  • NONomad

DefenceSolo3FDraft · Y11S3

Deny the Cocktail Lounge wall to Cocktail Balcony yourself and play from Bar.

The plan

  1. Put your denial on the Cocktail Lounge wall to Cocktail Balcony so it survives even if your randoms do nothing.
  2. Reinforce the Cocktail Lounge wall to Cocktail Balcony before anything else.
  3. Call "White Stairs" the moment you see a drone there.

Operators · first pick is you

  • KDKaid
  • Mute
  • Bandit
  • Mira

Common mistake

Roaming as a solo. Randoms rarely hold site while you are away.

DefenceDuo3FDraft · Y11S3

  2. Valkyrie cams cover White Stairs and Cocktail Entrance so you both see pushes early.
  3. Swap between Cocktail Lounge and Bar when one side gets hit, and call it before you move.

  • VAValkyrie
  • Bandit
  • Echo

AttackOperator setup3F

Where each attacker goes on Cocktail Lounge / Bar

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Cocktail Balcony

    Breach the Cocktail Lounge wall to Cocktail Balcony from Cocktail Balcony. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Cocktail Balcony

    Backup breach on the Cocktail Lounge wall to Cocktail Balcony. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Cocktail Balcony

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Cocktail Lounge wall to Cocktail Balcony. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Cocktail Balcony

    EMP the Cocktail Lounge wall to Cocktail Balcony right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · White Stairs

    Lance the denial on the Cocktail Lounge wall to Cocktail Balcony from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Bar.

  6. AS

    AshEntry · White Stairs

    Take White Stairs first and clear the doorway into Cocktail Lounge for the plant.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Cocktail Entrance

    Drive your shock drone into Bar and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · White Corridor

    Airjabs at White Corridor so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup3F

Where each defender goes on Cocktail Lounge / Bar

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Cocktail Lounge

    Claw the Cocktail Lounge wall to Cocktail Balcony from inside Cocktail Lounge so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Bar Freezer / Cocktail Entrance wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Cocktail Lounge

    Sit on the Cocktail Lounge wall to Cocktail Balcony with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Cocktail Lounge

    Jammer by the White Stairs door into Cocktail Lounge. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Bar

    Mirror the wall between Bar and Cocktail Lounge, so you can watch the plant in Cocktail Lounge from Bar without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Cocktail Lounge

    ADS on the Cocktail Entrance side of Cocktail Lounge. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · White Stairs

    Cams in White Stairs and Cocktail Entrance, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Bar

    Anchor Bar. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. VI

    VigilRoam · White Corridor

    Roam White Corridor and the rooms around it. Take one fight, then fall back to Bar by 1:00.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Cocktail Lounge wall to Cocktail Balcony from Cocktail Balcony.
  • Take White Stairs early and drone the site.
  • Clear White Corridor so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Cocktail Lounge wall to Cocktail Balcony stays denied, push White Stairs and Cocktail Entrance together with smokes and plant in Bar.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Cocktail Lounge wall to Cocktail Balcony first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Bar Freezer / Cocktail Entrance wall next.
  • Watch White Stairs and Cocktail Entrance. That is where the inside push comes from.

If it goes wrong

If the Cocktail Lounge wall to Cocktail Balcony opens early, fall back into Bar and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
