Party Room / Office

You plus four randoms
AttackSolo2FDraft · Y11S3

Bring your own breach or your own anti-denial.

The plan

  1. Ace or Maverick opens the Party Room wall to Bull even with no help.
  2. Drone Party Hallway for your randoms and call what you see.

Operators · first pick is you

  • ACAce
  • Maverick
  • Thatcher
  • Kali

Common mistake

Breaching without droning, so the denier just stays alive.

AttackDuo2FDraft · Y11S3

Breacher plus support on the wall.

The plan

  1. Thatcher drones the wall, then EMPs the denier.
  2. Thermite opens the Party Room wall to Bull and you both trade into Party Room.

Operators · first 2 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • Hibana
  • Kali

Common mistake

Support forgetting to drone again after the EMP.

AttackTrio2FDraft · Y11S3

Two on the wall, one pushing Party Hallway.

The plan

  1. Ash pushes Party Hallway to pull defenders off the wall.
  2. Open the Party Room wall to Bull while they are looking at Ash.

Operators · first 3 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • ASAsh

Common mistake

Entry going in before the wall is open and dying alone.

Attack4-stack2FDraft · Y11S3

Wall team, inside push and a flank watch.

The plan

  1. Nomad holds Shark Stairs so nobody hits your backs.
  2. Ace and Kali open the Party Room wall to Bull as Ash pushes Party Hallway.
  3. Plant in Party Room with the hole covering you.

Operators · first 4 are your squad

  • ACAce
  • KAKali
  • ASAsh
  • NONomad

Common mistake

Everyone on one side with nobody watching the flank.

Attack5-stack2FDraft · Y11S3

Full execute: wall, two pushes and a flank watch.

The plan

  1. 0:00 to 1:00: drone in and take Party Hallway.
  2. Thatcher EMPs and Thermite opens the Party Room wall to Bull at about 1:30.
  3. Ash and Ying hit Party Hallway and Garage Stairs together. Nomad holds Shark Stairs.

Operators · first 5 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • ASAsh
  • YIYing
  • NONomad

Common mistake

Opening the wall too early so defenders have time to reset.

DefenceSolo2FDraft · Y11S3

Deny the Party Room wall to Bull yourself and play from Office.

The plan

  1. Put your denial on the Party Room wall to Bull so it survives even if your randoms do nothing.
  2. Reinforce the Party Room wall to Bull before anything else.
  3. Call "Party Hallway" the moment you see a drone there.

Operators · first pick is you

  • KDKaid
  • Mute
  • Bandit
  • Mira

Common mistake

Roaming as a solo. Randoms rarely hold site while you are away.

DefenceDuo2FDraft · Y11S3

One anchor per room, one of you on cams.

The plan

  1. Kaid holds Party Room and keeps the Party Room wall to Bull electrified.
  2. Valkyrie cams cover Party Hallway and Garage Stairs so you both see pushes early.
  3. Swap between Party Room and Office when one side gets hit, and call it before you move.

Operators · first 2 are your squad

  • KDKaid
  • VAValkyrie
  • Bandit
  • Echo

Common mistake

Both of you watching the same doorway while the other room falls.

AttackOperator setup2F

Where each attacker goes on Party Room / Office

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Bull

    Breach the Party Room wall to Bull from Bull. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Bull

    Backup breach on the Party Room wall to Bull. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Bull

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Party Room wall to Bull. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Bull

    EMP the Party Room wall to Bull right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Party Hallway

    Lance the denial on the Party Room wall to Bull from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Office.

  6. AS

    AshEntry · Party Hallway

    Take Party Hallway first and clear the doorway into Party Room for the plant.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Garage Stairs

    Drive your shock drone into Office and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Shark Stairs

    Airjabs at Shark Stairs so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup2F

Where each defender goes on Party Room / Office

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Party Room

    Claw the Party Room wall to Bull from inside Party Room so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Supplies outside wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Party Room

    Sit on the Party Room wall to Bull with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Party Room

    Jammer by the Party Hallway door into Party Room. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Office

    Mirror the wall between Office and Party Room, so you can watch the plant in Party Room from Office without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Office

    ADS on the Garage Stairs side of Office. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Party Hallway

    Cams in Party Hallway and Garage Stairs, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Office

    Anchor Office. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. VI

    VigilRoam · Shark Stairs

    Roam Shark Stairs and the rooms around it. Take one fight, then fall back to Office by 1:00.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Party Room wall to Bull from Bull.
  • Take Party Hallway early and drone the site.
  • Clear Shark Stairs so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Party Room wall to Bull stays denied, push Party Hallway and Garage Stairs together with smokes and plant in Office.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Party Room wall to Bull first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Supplies outside wall next.
  • Watch Party Hallway and Garage Stairs. That is where the inside push comes from.

If it goes wrong

If the Party Room wall to Bull opens early, fall back into Office and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
