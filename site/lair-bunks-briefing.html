Bunks / Briefing

Best guess The two bomb rooms on this plan are our best guess from the blueprint. Check it in a custom game before you rely on it.

You plus four randoms
AttackSolo1FDraft · Y11S3

Play vertical yourself. It works without team help.

The plan

  1. Get to Storage above Briefing and open holes over common anchor spots.
  2. Drone Bunks Terrace for your randoms and call what you see.

  • Zofia

AttackOperator setup1F

Where each attacker goes on Bunks / Briefing

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Green Hallway

    Breach the Briefing wall to Green Hallway from Green Hallway. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Green Hallway

    Backup breach on the Briefing wall to Green Hallway. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Green Hallway

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Briefing wall to Green Hallway. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Green Hallway

    EMP the Briefing wall to Green Hallway right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Bunks Terrace

    Lance the denial on the Briefing wall to Green Hallway from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Bunks.

  6. BU

    BuckVertical · 2F above

    Play Storage above Briefing. Open small holes over the default anchor spots in Briefing.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Green Hallway

    Drive your shock drone into Bunks and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · North Dock Stairs

    Airjabs at North Dock Stairs so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup1F

Where each defender goes on Bunks / Briefing

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Briefing

    Claw the Briefing wall to Green Hallway from inside Briefing so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the outside wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Briefing

    Sit on the Briefing wall to Green Hallway with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Bunks

    Jammer by the Bunks Terrace door into Bunks. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Bunks

    Mirror the wall between Bunks and Briefing, so you can watch the plant in Briefing from Bunks without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Briefing

    ADS on the Green Hallway side of Briefing. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Bunks Terrace

    Cams in Bunks Terrace and Green Hallway, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Bunks

    Anchor Bunks. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. PU

    PulseVertical hunt · 2F above

    Go up to Storage above Briefing early. Pulse attackers setting up vertical and pick them off before they open the floor.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Briefing wall to Green Hallway from Green Hallway.
  • Play vertical from Storage above Briefing to pin the anchors.
  • Clear North Dock Stairs so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Briefing wall to Green Hallway stays denied, push Bunks Terrace and Green Hallway together with smokes and plant in Bunks.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Briefing wall to Green Hallway first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the outside wall next.
  • Watch Storage above Briefing. Attackers will play vertical from there.

If it goes wrong

If the Briefing wall to Green Hallway opens early, fall back into Bunks and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
