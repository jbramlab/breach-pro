Master Office / R6 Room

Best guess The two bomb rooms on this plan are our best guess from the blueprint. Check it in a custom game before you rely on it.

You plus four randoms
AttackSolo2FDraft · Y11S3

Bring your own breach or your own anti-denial.

The plan

  1. Ace or Maverick opens the R6 Room outside wall even with no help.
  2. Drone Courtyard Balcony for your randoms and call what you see.

Operators · first pick is you

  • ACAce
  • Maverick
  • Thatcher
  • Kali

Common mistake

Breaching without droning, so the denier just stays alive.

Breacher plus support on the wall.

The plan

  2. Thermite opens the R6 Room outside wall and you both trade into R6 Room.

Operators · first 2 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • Hibana
  • Kali

Common mistake

Support forgetting to drone again after the EMP.

Two on the wall, one pushing Courtyard Balcony.

  2. Open the R6 Room outside wall while they are looking at Ash.

Operators · first 3 are your squad

DefenceSolo2FDraft · Y11S3

Deny the R6 Room outside wall yourself and play from Master Office.

The plan

  2. Reinforce the R6 Room outside wall before anything else.
  3. Call "Courtyard Balcony" the moment you see a drone there.

  • Mute
  • Bandit
  • Mira

AttackOperator setup2F

Where each attacker goes on Master Office / R6 Room

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Outside

    Breach the R6 Room outside wall from Outside. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Outside

    Backup breach on the R6 Room outside wall. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Outside

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the R6 Room outside wall. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Outside

    EMP the R6 Room outside wall right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Courtyard Balcony

    Lance the denial on the R6 Room outside wall from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Master Office.

  6. AS

    AshEntry · Courtyard Balcony

    Take Courtyard Balcony first and clear the doorway into Master Office for the plant.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Mask

    Drive your shock drone into Master Office and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Courtyard

    Airjabs at Courtyard so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup2F

Where each defender goes on Master Office / R6 Room

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · R6 Room

    Claw the R6 Room outside wall from inside R6 Room so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Courtyard Balcony / Mask wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · R6 Room

    Sit on the R6 Room outside wall with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Master Office

    Jammer by the Courtyard Balcony door into Master Office. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Master Office

    Mirror the wall between Master Office and R6 Room, so you can watch the plant in R6 Room from Master Office without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Master Office

    ADS on the Mask side of Master Office. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Courtyard Balcony

    Cams in Courtyard Balcony and Mask, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Master Office

    Anchor Master Office. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. VI

    VigilRoam · Courtyard

    Roam Courtyard and the rooms around it. Take one fight, then fall back to Master Office by 1:00.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the R6 Room outside wall from Outside.
  • Take Courtyard Balcony early and drone the site.
  • Clear Courtyard so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the R6 Room outside wall stays denied, push Courtyard Balcony and Mask together with smokes and plant in Master Office.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the R6 Room outside wall first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Courtyard Balcony / Mask wall next.
  • Watch Courtyard Balcony and Mask. That is where the inside push comes from.

If it goes wrong

If the R6 Room outside wall opens early, fall back into Master Office and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
