Armory Lockers / Archives

You plus four randoms
AttackSolo2FDraft · Y11S3

Bring your own breach or your own anti-denial.

The plan

  1. Ace or Maverick opens the Archives wall to Side room even with no help.
  2. Drone Offices Hallway for your randoms and call what you see.

Operators · first pick is you

  • ACAce
  • Maverick
  • Thatcher
  • Kali

Common mistake

Breaching without droning, so the denier just stays alive.

DefenceSolo2FDraft · Y11S3

Deny the Archives wall to Side room yourself and play from Archives.

The plan

  1. Put your denial on the Archives wall to Side room so it survives even if your randoms do nothing.
  2. Reinforce the Archives wall to Side room before anything else.
  3. Call "Offices Hallway" the moment you see a drone there.

Operators · first pick is you

  • KDKaid
  • Mute
  • Bandit
  • Mira

Common mistake

Roaming as a solo. Randoms rarely hold site while you are away.

AttackOperator setup2F

Where each attacker goes on Armory Lockers / Archives

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Side room

    Breach the Archives wall to Side room from Side room. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Side room

    Backup breach on the Archives wall to Side room. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Side room

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Archives wall to Side room. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Side room

    EMP the Archives wall to Side room right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Offices Hallway

    Lance the denial on the Archives wall to Side room from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Armory Lockers.

  6. AS

    AshEntry · Offices Hallway

    Take Offices Hallway first and clear the doorway into Archives for the plant.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Sandwich

    Drive your shock drone into Armory Lockers and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Offices

    Airjabs at Offices so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup2F

Where each defender goes on Armory Lockers / Archives

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Archives

    Claw the Archives wall to Side room from inside Archives so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Cafeteria / Red Hallway wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Archives

    Sit on the Archives wall to Side room with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Archives

    Jammer by the Offices Hallway door into Archives. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Armory Lockers

    Mirror the wall between Armory Lockers and Archives, so you can watch the plant in Archives from Armory Lockers without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Archives

    ADS on the Sandwich side of Archives. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Offices Hallway

    Cams in Offices Hallway and Sandwich, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Armory Lockers

    Anchor Armory Lockers. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. VI

    VigilRoam · Offices

    Roam Offices and the rooms around it. Take one fight, then fall back to Armory Lockers by 1:00.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Archives wall to Side room from Side room.
  • Take Offices Hallway early and drone the site.
  • Clear Offices so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Archives wall to Side room stays denied, push Offices Hallway and Sandwich together with smokes and plant in Archives.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Archives wall to Side room first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Cafeteria / Red Hallway wall next.
  • Watch Offices Hallway and Sandwich. That is where the inside push comes from.

If it goes wrong

If the Archives wall to Side room opens early, fall back into Archives and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
