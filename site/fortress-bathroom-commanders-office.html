Bathroom / Commander's Office

Best guess The two bomb rooms on this plan are our best guess from the blueprint. Check it in a custom game before you rely on it.

You plus four randoms
AttackSolo2FDraft · Y11S3

Bring your own breach or your own anti-denial.

The plan

  1. Ace or Maverick opens the Bathroom wall to Bedroom even with no help.
  2. Drone Renovation Hallway for your randoms and call what you see.

Operators · first pick is you

  • ACAce
  • Maverick
  • Thatcher
  • Kali

Common mistake

Breaching without droning, so the denier just stays alive.

  • Kali

  3. Call "Renovation Hallway" the moment you see a drone there.

AttackOperator setup2F

Where each attacker goes on Bathroom / Commander's Office

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Bedroom

    Breach the Bathroom wall to Bedroom from Bedroom. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Bedroom

    Backup breach on the Bathroom wall to Bedroom. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Bedroom

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Bathroom wall to Bedroom. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Bedroom

    EMP the Bathroom wall to Bedroom right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Renovation Hallway

    Lance the denial on the Bathroom wall to Bedroom from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Commander's Office.

  6. AS

    AshEntry · Renovation Hallway

    Take Renovation Hallway first and clear the doorway into Commander's Office for the plant.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Renovation

    Drive your shock drone into Commander's Office and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Bedroom

    Airjabs at Bedroom so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup2F

Where each defender goes on Bathroom / Commander's Office

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Bathroom

    Claw the Bathroom wall to Bedroom from inside Bathroom so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Renovation Hallway / Renovation wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Bathroom

    Sit on the Bathroom wall to Bedroom with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Commander's Office

    Jammer by the Renovation Hallway door into Commander's Office. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Commander's Office

    Mirror the wall between Commander's Office and Bathroom, so you can watch the plant in Bathroom from Commander's Office without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Commander's Office

    ADS on the Renovation side of Commander's Office. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Renovation Hallway

    Cams in Renovation Hallway and Renovation, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Commander's Office

    Anchor Commander's Office. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. VI

    VigilRoam · Bedroom

    Roam Bedroom and the rooms around it. Take one fight, then fall back to Commander's Office by 1:00.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Bathroom wall to Bedroom from Bedroom.
  • Take Renovation Hallway early and drone the site.
  • Clear Bedroom so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Bathroom wall to Bedroom stays denied, push Renovation Hallway and Renovation together with smokes and plant in Bathroom.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Bathroom wall to Bedroom first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Renovation Hallway / Renovation wall next.
  • Watch Renovation Hallway and Renovation. That is where the inside push comes from.

If it goes wrong

If the Bathroom wall to Bedroom opens early, fall back into Bathroom and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
