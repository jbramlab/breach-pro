Best guess The two bomb rooms on this plan are our best guess from the blueprint. Check it in a custom game before you rely on it.

AttackOperator setup2F

Where each attacker goes on Cigar Room / Pool

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Jukebox

    Breach the Pool wall to Jukebox from Jukebox. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Jukebox

    Backup breach on the Pool wall to Jukebox. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Jukebox

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Pool wall to Jukebox. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Jukebox

    EMP the Pool wall to Jukebox right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Kitchen

    Lance the denial on the Pool wall to Jukebox from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Cigar Room.

  6. AS

    AshEntry · Kitchen

    Take Kitchen first and clear the doorway into Cigar Room for the plant.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Swamp

    Drive your shock drone into Cigar Room and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Apples

    Airjabs at Apples so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup2F

Where each defender goes on Cigar Room / Pool

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Pool

    Claw the Pool wall to Jukebox from inside Pool so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Credit / Fish wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Pool

    Sit on the Pool wall to Jukebox with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Cigar Room

    Jammer by the Kitchen door into Cigar Room. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Cigar Room

    Mirror the wall between Cigar Room and Pool, so you can watch the plant in Pool from Cigar Room without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Cigar Room

    ADS on the Swamp side of Cigar Room. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Kitchen

    Cams in Kitchen and Swamp, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Cigar Room

    Anchor Cigar Room. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. VI

    VigilRoam · Apples

    Roam Apples and the rooms around it. Take one fight, then fall back to Cigar Room by 1:00.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Pool wall to Jukebox from Jukebox.
  • Take Kitchen early and drone the site.
  • Clear Apples so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Pool wall to Jukebox stays denied, push Kitchen and Swamp together with smokes and plant in Pool.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Pool wall to Jukebox first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Credit / Fish wall next.
  • Watch Kitchen and Swamp. That is where the inside push comes from.

If it goes wrong

If the Pool wall to Jukebox opens early, fall back into Pool and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
