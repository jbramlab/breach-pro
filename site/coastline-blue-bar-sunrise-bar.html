Blue Bar / Sunrise Bar

Best guess The two bomb rooms on this plan are our best guess from the blueprint. Check it in a custom game before you rely on it.

AttackOperator setup1F

Where each attacker goes on Blue Bar / Sunrise Bar

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Outside

    Breach the Blue Bar outside wall from Outside. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Outside

    Backup breach on the Blue Bar outside wall. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Outside

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Blue Bar outside wall. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Outside

    EMP the Blue Bar outside wall right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Kitchen Hallway

    Lance the denial on the Blue Bar outside wall from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Sunrise Bar.

  6. BU

    BuckVertical · 2F above

    Play Hookah Lounge above Blue Bar. Open small holes over the default anchor spots in Blue Bar.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Blue Stairs

    Drive your shock drone into Sunrise Bar and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Kitchen

    Airjabs at Kitchen so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup1F

Where each defender goes on Blue Bar / Sunrise Bar

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Blue Bar

    Claw the Blue Bar outside wall from inside Blue Bar so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Sunrise Entrance outside wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Blue Bar

    Sit on the Blue Bar outside wall with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Sunrise Bar

    Jammer by the Kitchen Hallway door into Sunrise Bar. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Sunrise Bar

    Mirror the wall between Sunrise Bar and Blue Bar, so you can watch the plant in Blue Bar from Sunrise Bar without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Blue Bar

    ADS on the Blue Stairs side of Blue Bar. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Kitchen Hallway

    Cams in Kitchen Hallway and Blue Stairs, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Sunrise Bar

    Anchor Sunrise Bar. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. PU

    PulseVertical hunt · 2F above

    Go up to Hookah Lounge above Blue Bar early. Pulse attackers setting up vertical and pick them off before they open the floor.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Blue Bar outside wall from Outside.
  • Play vertical from Hookah Lounge above Blue Bar to pin the anchors.
  • Clear Kitchen so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Blue Bar outside wall stays denied, push Kitchen Hallway and Blue Stairs together with smokes and plant in Sunrise Bar.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Blue Bar outside wall first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Sunrise Entrance outside wall next.
  • Watch Hookah Lounge above Blue Bar. Attackers will play vertical from there.

If it goes wrong

If the Blue Bar outside wall opens early, fall back into Sunrise Bar and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
