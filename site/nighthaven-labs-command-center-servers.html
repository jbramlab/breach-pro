Command Center / Servers

You plus four randoms
AttackSolo2FDraft · Y11S3

Bring your own breach or your own anti-denial.

The plan

  1. Ace or Maverick opens the Servers wall to Connector even with no help.
  2. Drone Command Hallway for your randoms and call what you see.

Operators · first pick is you

  • ACAce
  • Maverick
  • Thatcher
  • Kali

Common mistake

Breaching without droning, so the denier just stays alive.

AttackDuo2FDraft · Y11S3

Breacher plus support on the wall.

The plan

  1. Thatcher drones the wall, then EMPs the denier.
  2. Thermite opens the Servers wall to Connector and you both trade into Command Center.

Operators · first 2 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • Hibana
  • Kali

Common mistake

Support forgetting to drone again after the EMP.

AttackTrio2FDraft · Y11S3

Two on the wall, one pushing Command Hallway.

The plan

  1. Ash pushes Command Hallway to pull defenders off the wall.
  2. Open the Servers wall to Connector while they are looking at Ash.

Operators · first 3 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • ASAsh

Common mistake

Wall team, inside push and a flank watch.

The plan

  2. Ace and Kali open the Servers wall to Connector as Ash pushes Command Hallway.
  3. Plant in Command Center with the hole covering you.

Operators · first 4 are your squad

  • ACAce
  • KAKali
  • ASAsh
  • NONomad

Attack5-stack2FDraft · Y11S3

Full execute: wall, two pushes and a flank watch.

The plan

  1. 0:00 to 1:00: drone in and take Command Hallway.
  2. Thatcher EMPs and Thermite opens the Servers wall to Connector at about 1:30.
  3. Ash and Ying hit Command Hallway and Blue Stairs together. Nomad holds Top Blue.

Operators · first 5 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • ASAsh
  • YIYing

DefenceSolo2FDraft · Y11S3

Deny the Servers wall to Connector yourself and play from Servers.

The plan

  1. Put your denial on the Servers wall to Connector so it survives even if your randoms do nothing.
  2. Reinforce the Servers wall to Connector before anything else.
  3. Call "Command Hallway" the moment you see a drone there.

Operators · first pick is you

  • KDKaid
  • Mute
  • Bandit
  • Mira

Common mistake

Roaming as a solo. Randoms rarely hold site while you are away.

DefenceDuo2FDraft · Y11S3

One anchor per room, one of you on cams.

The plan

  2. Valkyrie cams cover Command Hallway and Blue Stairs so you both see pushes early.
  3. Swap between Command Center and Servers when one side gets hit, and call it before you move.

  • KDKaid
  • VAValkyrie
  • Bandit
  • Echo

AttackOperator setup2F

Where each attacker goes on Command Center / Servers

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Connector

    Breach the Servers wall to Connector from Connector. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Connector

    Backup breach on the Servers wall to Connector. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Connector

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Servers wall to Connector. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Connector

    EMP the Servers wall to Connector right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Command Hallway

    Lance the denial on the Servers wall to Connector from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Command Center.

  6. AS

    AshEntry · Command Hallway

    Take Command Hallway first and clear the doorway into Command Center for the plant.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Blue Stairs

    Drive your shock drone into Command Center and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Top Blue

    Airjabs at Top Blue so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup2F

Where each defender goes on Command Center / Servers

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Servers

    Claw the Servers wall to Connector from inside Servers so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the IT outside wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Servers

    Sit on the Servers wall to Connector with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Command Center

    Jammer by the Command Hallway door into Command Center. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Command Center

    Mirror the wall between Command Center and Servers, so you can watch the plant in Servers from Command Center without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Servers

    ADS on the Blue Stairs side of Servers. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Command Hallway

    Cams in Command Hallway and Blue Stairs, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Command Center

    Anchor Command Center. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. VI

    VigilRoam · Top Blue

    Roam Top Blue and the rooms around it. Take one fight, then fall back to Command Center by 1:00.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Servers wall to Connector from Connector.
  • Take Command Hallway early and drone the site.
  • Clear Top Blue so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Servers wall to Connector stays denied, push Command Hallway and Blue Stairs together with smokes and plant in Servers.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Servers wall to Connector first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the IT outside wall next.
  • Watch Command Hallway and Blue Stairs. That is where the inside push comes from.

If it goes wrong

If the Servers wall to Connector opens early, fall back into Servers and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
