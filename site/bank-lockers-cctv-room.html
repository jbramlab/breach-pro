Lockers / CCTV Room

You plus four randoms
AttackSoloBDraft · Y11S3

Bring your own breach or your own anti-denial.

The plan

  1. Ace or Maverick opens the Lockers wall to Vault Entrance even with no help.
  2. Drone Vault Lobby for your randoms and call what you see.

Operators · first pick is you

  • ACAce
  • Maverick
  • Thatcher
  • Kali

Common mistake

Breaching without droning, so the denier just stays alive.

Breacher plus support on the wall.

The plan

  2. Thermite opens the Lockers wall to Vault Entrance and you both trade into Lockers.

Operators · first 2 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • Hibana
  • Kali

Common mistake

Support forgetting to drone again after the EMP.

Operators · first 3 are your squad

  • ASAsh

  3. Plant in Lockers with the hole covering you.

DefenceSoloBDraft · Y11S3

Deny the Lockers wall to Vault Entrance yourself and play from CCTV Room.

The plan

  1. Put your denial on the Lockers wall to Vault Entrance so it survives even if your randoms do nothing.
  2. Reinforce the Lockers wall to Vault Entrance before anything else.
  3. Call "Vault Lobby" the moment you see a drone there.

Operators · first pick is you

  • KDKaid
  • Mute
  • Bandit
  • Mira

Common mistake

Roaming as a solo. Randoms rarely hold site while you are away.

  2. Valkyrie cams cover Vault Lobby and Vault Entrance so you both see pushes early.
  3. Swap between Lockers and CCTV Room when one side gets hit, and call it before you move.

AttackOperator setupB

Where each attacker goes on Lockers / CCTV Room

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Vault Entrance

    Breach the Lockers wall to Vault Entrance from Vault Entrance. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Vault Entrance

    Backup breach on the Lockers wall to Vault Entrance. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Vault Entrance

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Lockers wall to Vault Entrance. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Vault Entrance

    EMP the Lockers wall to Vault Entrance right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Vault Lobby

    Lance the denial on the Lockers wall to Vault Entrance from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in CCTV Room.

  6. AS

    AshEntry · Vault Lobby

    Take Vault Lobby first and clear the doorway into Lockers for the plant.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Vault Entrance

    Drive your shock drone into CCTV Room and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Elevators

    Airjabs at Elevators so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setupB

Where each defender goes on Lockers / CCTV Room

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Lockers

    Claw the Lockers wall to Vault Entrance from inside Lockers so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Lockers / Vault Lobby wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Lockers

    Sit on the Lockers wall to Vault Entrance with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Lockers

    Jammer by the Vault Lobby door into Lockers. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · CCTV Room

    Mirror the wall between CCTV Room and Lockers, so you can watch the plant in Lockers from CCTV Room without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Lockers

    ADS on the Vault Entrance side of Lockers. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Vault Lobby

    Cams in Vault Lobby and Vault Entrance, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · CCTV Room

    Anchor CCTV Room. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. VI

    VigilRoam · Elevators

    Roam Elevators and the rooms around it. Take one fight, then fall back to CCTV Room by 1:00.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Lockers wall to Vault Entrance from Vault Entrance.
  • Take Vault Lobby early and drone the site.
  • Clear Elevators so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Lockers wall to Vault Entrance stays denied, push Vault Lobby and Vault Entrance together with smokes and plant in CCTV Room.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Lockers wall to Vault Entrance first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Lockers / Vault Lobby wall next.
  • Watch Vault Lobby and Vault Entrance. That is where the inside push comes from.

If it goes wrong

If the Lockers wall to Vault Entrance opens early, fall back into CCTV Room and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
