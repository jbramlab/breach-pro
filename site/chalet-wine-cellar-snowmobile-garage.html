Wine Cellar / Snowmobile Garage

You plus four randoms
AttackSoloBDraft · Y11S3

Play vertical yourself. It works without team help.

The plan

  1. Get to Bar above Snowmobile Garage and open holes over common anchor spots.
  2. Drone Basement Hallway for your randoms and call what you see.

Operators · first pick is you

  • BUBuck
  • Sledge
  • Ace
  • Zofia

Common mistake

Opening holes you never actually play from.

DefenceSoloBDraft · Y11S3

Deny the Snowmobile Garage outside wall yourself and play from Wine Cellar.

The plan

  1. Put your denial on the Snowmobile Garage outside wall so it survives even if your randoms do nothing.
  2. Reinforce the Snowmobile Garage outside wall before anything else.
  3. Call "Basement Hallway" the moment you see a drone there.

Operators · first pick is you

  • KDKaid
  • Mute
  • Bandit
  • Mira

Common mistake

Roaming as a solo. Randoms rarely hold site while you are away.

  2. Valkyrie cams cover Basement Hallway and Main Stairs so you both see pushes early.
  3. Swap between Wine Cellar and Snowmobile Garage when one side gets hit, and call it before you move.

AttackOperator setupB

Where each attacker goes on Wine Cellar / Snowmobile Garage

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Outside

    Breach the Snowmobile Garage outside wall from Outside. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Outside

    Backup breach on the Snowmobile Garage outside wall. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Outside

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Snowmobile Garage outside wall. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Outside

    EMP the Snowmobile Garage outside wall right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Basement Hallway

    Lance the denial on the Snowmobile Garage outside wall from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Wine Cellar.

  6. BU

    BuckVertical · 1F above

    Play Bar above Snowmobile Garage. Open small holes over the default anchor spots in Snowmobile Garage.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Main Stairs

    Drive your shock drone into Wine Cellar and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Backyard Stairs

    Airjabs at Backyard Stairs so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setupB

Where each defender goes on Wine Cellar / Snowmobile Garage

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Snowmobile Garage

    Claw the Snowmobile Garage outside wall from inside Snowmobile Garage so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Basement Hallway / Main Stairs wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Snowmobile Garage

    Sit on the Snowmobile Garage outside wall with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Wine Cellar

    Jammer by the Basement Hallway door into Wine Cellar. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Wine Cellar

    Mirror the wall between Wine Cellar and Snowmobile Garage, so you can watch the plant in Snowmobile Garage from Wine Cellar without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Wine Cellar

    ADS on the Main Stairs side of Wine Cellar. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Basement Hallway

    Cams in Basement Hallway and Main Stairs, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Wine Cellar

    Anchor Wine Cellar. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. PU

    PulseVertical hunt · 1F above

    Go up to Bar above Snowmobile Garage early. Pulse attackers setting up vertical and pick them off before they open the floor.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Snowmobile Garage outside wall from Outside.
  • Play vertical from Bar above Snowmobile Garage to pin the anchors.
  • Clear Backyard Stairs so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Snowmobile Garage outside wall stays denied, push Basement Hallway and Main Stairs together with smokes and plant in Wine Cellar.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Snowmobile Garage outside wall first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Basement Hallway / Main Stairs wall next.
  • Watch Bar above Snowmobile Garage. Attackers will play vertical from there.

If it goes wrong

If the Snowmobile Garage outside wall opens early, fall back into Wine Cellar and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
