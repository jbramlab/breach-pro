Laundry Room / Supply Room

You plus four randoms
AttackSoloBDraft · Y11S3

Play vertical yourself. It works without team help.

The plan

  1. Get to Meeting Hall above Supply Room and open holes over common anchor spots.
  2. Drone Basement Corridor for your randoms and call what you see.

  2. Reinforce the Laundry Room wall to Closet before anything else.
  3. Call "Basement Corridor" the moment you see a drone there.

AttackOperator setupB

Where each attacker goes on Laundry Room / Supply Room

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Closet

    Breach the Laundry Room wall to Closet from Closet. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Closet

    Backup breach on the Laundry Room wall to Closet. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Closet

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Laundry Room wall to Closet. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Closet

    EMP the Laundry Room wall to Closet right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Basement Corridor

    Lance the denial on the Laundry Room wall to Closet from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Supply Room.

  6. BU

    BuckVertical · 1F above

    Play Meeting Hall above Supply Room. Open small holes over the default anchor spots in Supply Room.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Laundry Stairs

    Drive your shock drone into Supply Room and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Closet

    Airjabs at Closet so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setupB

Where each defender goes on Laundry Room / Supply Room

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Laundry Room

    Claw the Laundry Room wall to Closet from inside Laundry Room so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Blue / Supply Room wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Laundry Room

    Sit on the Laundry Room wall to Closet with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Supply Room

    Jammer by the Basement Corridor door into Supply Room. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Supply Room

    Mirror the wall between Supply Room and Laundry Room, so you can watch the plant in Laundry Room from Supply Room without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Laundry Room

    ADS on the Laundry Stairs side of Laundry Room. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Basement Corridor

    Cams in Basement Corridor and Laundry Stairs, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Supply Room

    Anchor Supply Room. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. PU

    PulseVertical hunt · 1F above

    Go up to Meeting Hall above Supply Room early. Pulse attackers setting up vertical and pick them off before they open the floor.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Laundry Room wall to Closet from Closet.
  • Play vertical from Meeting Hall above Supply Room to pin the anchors.
  • Clear Closet so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Laundry Room wall to Closet stays denied, push Basement Corridor and Laundry Stairs together with smokes and plant in Supply Room.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Laundry Room wall to Closet first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Blue / Supply Room wall next.
  • Watch Meeting Hall above Supply Room. Attackers will play vertical from there.

If it goes wrong

If the Laundry Room wall to Closet opens early, fall back into Supply Room and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
