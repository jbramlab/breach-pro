AttackOperator setup1F

Where each attacker goes on Hammam / Sitting Room

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Changing

    Breach the Hammam wall to Changing from Changing. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Changing

    Backup breach on the Hammam wall to Changing. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Changing

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Hammam wall to Changing. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Changing

    EMP the Hammam wall to Changing right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Kitchen Hallway

    Lance the denial on the Hammam wall to Changing from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Sitting Room.

  6. BU

    BuckVertical · 2F above

    Play Portrait above Sitting Room. Open small holes over the default anchor spots in Sitting Room.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Main Stairs

    Drive your shock drone into Sitting Room and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Gun Room

    Airjabs at Gun Room so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup1F

Where each defender goes on Hammam / Sitting Room

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Hammam

    Claw the Hammam wall to Changing from inside Hammam so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Gun Room / Changing wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Hammam

    Sit on the Hammam wall to Changing with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Hammam

    Jammer by the Kitchen Hallway door into Hammam. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Sitting Room

    Mirror the wall between Sitting Room and Hammam, so you can watch the plant in Hammam from Sitting Room without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Hammam

    ADS on the Main Stairs side of Hammam. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Kitchen Hallway

    Cams in Kitchen Hallway and Main Stairs, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Sitting Room

    Anchor Sitting Room. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. PU

    PulseVertical hunt · 2F above

    Go up to Portrait above Sitting Room early. Pulse attackers setting up vertical and pick them off before they open the floor.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Hammam wall to Changing from Changing.
  • Play vertical from Portrait above Sitting Room to pin the anchors.
  • Clear Gun Room so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Hammam wall to Changing stays denied, push Kitchen Hallway and Main Stairs together with smokes and plant in Sitting Room.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Hammam wall to Changing first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Gun Room / Changing wall next.
  • Watch Portrait above Sitting Room. Attackers will play vertical from there.

If it goes wrong

If the Hammam wall to Changing opens early, fall back into Sitting Room and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
