Best guess The two bomb rooms on this plan are our best guess from the blueprint. Check it in a custom game before you rely on it.

  2. Drone Tellers Hallway for your randoms and call what you see.

AttackOperator setup1F

Where each attacker goes on Tellers / Servers

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Lounge

    Breach the Tellers wall to Lounge from Lounge. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Lounge

    Backup breach on the Tellers wall to Lounge. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Lounge

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Tellers wall to Lounge. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Lounge

    EMP the Tellers wall to Lounge right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Tellers Hallway

    Lance the denial on the Tellers wall to Lounge from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Visa.

  6. BU

    BuckVertical · 2F above

    Play Admin above Visa. Open small holes over the default anchor spots in Visa.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Visa Hallway

    Drive your shock drone into Visa and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Server Stairs

    Airjabs at Server Stairs so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup1F

Where each defender goes on Tellers / Servers

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Tellers

    Claw the Tellers wall to Lounge from inside Tellers so the whole wall is live.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Tellers

    Sit on the Tellers wall to Lounge with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Tellers

    Jammer by the Tellers Hallway door into Tellers. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Visa

    Mirror the wall between Visa and Tellers, so you can watch the plant in Tellers from Visa without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Tellers

    ADS on the Visa Hallway side of Tellers. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Tellers Hallway

    Cams in Tellers Hallway and Visa Hallway, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Visa

    Anchor Visa. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. PU

    PulseVertical hunt · 2F above

    Go up to Admin above Visa early. Pulse attackers setting up vertical and pick them off before they open the floor.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Tellers wall to Lounge from Lounge.
  • Play vertical from Admin above Visa to pin the anchors.
  • Clear Server Stairs so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Tellers wall to Lounge stays denied, push Tellers Hallway and Visa Hallway together with smokes and plant in Visa.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Tellers wall to Lounge first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Keep the way between Tellers and Visa open so anchors can rotate.
  • Watch Admin above Visa. Attackers will play vertical from there.

If it goes wrong

If the Tellers wall to Lounge opens early, fall back into Visa and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
