Supply Room / Customs Inspection

You plus four randoms
AttackSolo1FDraft · Y11S3

Play vertical yourself. It works without team help.

The plan

  1. Get to CCTV above Customs Inspection and open holes over common anchor spots.
  2. Drone Supply Corridor for your randoms and call what you see.

  • Sledge
  • Ace
  • Zofia

DefenceSolo1FDraft · Y11S3

Deny the Supply Room wall to Main Stairs yourself and play from Customs Inspection.

The plan

  1. Put your denial on the Supply Room wall to Main Stairs so it survives even if your randoms do nothing.
  2. Reinforce the Supply Room wall to Main Stairs before anything else.
  3. Call "Supply Corridor" the moment you see a drone there.

  • Bandit
  • Mira

AttackOperator setup1F

Where each attacker goes on Supply Room / Customs Inspection

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Main Stairs

    Breach the Supply Room wall to Main Stairs from Main Stairs. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Main Stairs

    Backup breach on the Supply Room wall to Main Stairs. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Main Stairs

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Supply Room wall to Main Stairs. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Main Stairs

    EMP the Supply Room wall to Main Stairs right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Supply Corridor

    Lance the denial on the Supply Room wall to Main Stairs from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Customs Inspection.

  6. BU

    BuckVertical · 2F above

    Play CCTV above Customs Inspection. Open small holes over the default anchor spots in Customs Inspection.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Customs Desk

    Drive your shock drone into Customs Inspection and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Main Stairs

    Airjabs at Main Stairs so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup1F

Where each defender goes on Supply Room / Customs Inspection

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Supply Room

    Claw the Supply Room wall to Main Stairs from inside Supply Room so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Customs Desk / side room wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Supply Room

    Sit on the Supply Room wall to Main Stairs with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Supply Room

    Jammer by the Supply Corridor door into Supply Room. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Customs Inspection

    Mirror the wall between Customs Inspection and Supply Room, so you can watch the plant in Supply Room from Customs Inspection without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Customs Inspection

    ADS on the Customs Desk side of Customs Inspection. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Supply Corridor

    Cams in Supply Corridor and Customs Desk, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Customs Inspection

    Anchor Customs Inspection. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. PU

    PulseVertical hunt · 2F above

    Go up to CCTV above Customs Inspection early. Pulse attackers setting up vertical and pick them off before they open the floor.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Supply Room wall to Main Stairs from Main Stairs.
  • Play vertical from CCTV above Customs Inspection to pin the anchors.
  • Clear Main Stairs so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Supply Room wall to Main Stairs stays denied, push Supply Corridor and Customs Desk together with smokes and plant in Customs Inspection.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Supply Room wall to Main Stairs first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Customs Desk / side room wall next.
  • Watch CCTV above Customs Inspection. Attackers will play vertical from there.

If it goes wrong

If the Supply Room wall to Main Stairs opens early, fall back into Customs Inspection and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
