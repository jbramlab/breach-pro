AttackOperator setup1F

Where each attacker goes on Workshop / Ventilation Room

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Bathroom

    Breach the Workshop wall to Bathroom from Bathroom. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Bathroom

    Backup breach on the Workshop wall to Bathroom. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Bathroom

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Workshop wall to Bathroom. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Bathroom

    EMP the Workshop wall to Bathroom right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Bathroom Hallway

    Lance the denial on the Workshop wall to Bathroom from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Ventilation Room.

  6. BU

    BuckVertical · 2F above

    Play Armory Lockers above Ventilation Room. Open small holes over the default anchor spots in Ventilation Room.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Lobby

    Drive your shock drone into Ventilation Room and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Main Stairs

    Airjabs at Main Stairs so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup1F

Where each defender goes on Workshop / Ventilation Room

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Workshop

    Claw the Workshop wall to Bathroom from inside Workshop so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Detention outside wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Workshop

    Sit on the Workshop wall to Bathroom with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Workshop

    Jammer by the Bathroom Hallway door into Workshop. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Ventilation Room

    Mirror the wall between Ventilation Room and Workshop, so you can watch the plant in Workshop from Ventilation Room without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Workshop

    ADS on the Lobby side of Workshop. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Bathroom Hallway

    Cams in Bathroom Hallway and Lobby, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Ventilation Room

    Anchor Ventilation Room. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. PU

    PulseVertical hunt · 2F above

    Go up to Armory Lockers above Ventilation Room early. Pulse attackers setting up vertical and pick them off before they open the floor.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Workshop wall to Bathroom from Bathroom.
  • Play vertical from Armory Lockers above Ventilation Room to pin the anchors.
  • Clear Main Stairs so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Workshop wall to Bathroom stays denied, push Bathroom Hallway and Lobby together with smokes and plant in Ventilation Room.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Workshop wall to Bathroom first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Detention outside wall next.
  • Watch Armory Lockers above Ventilation Room. Attackers will play vertical from there.

If it goes wrong

If the Workshop wall to Bathroom opens early, fall back into Ventilation Room and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
