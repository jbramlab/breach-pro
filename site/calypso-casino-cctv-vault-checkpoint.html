AttackOperator setupB

Where each attacker goes on CCTV / Vault Checkpoint

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Cash

    Breach the CCTV wall to Cash from Cash. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Cash

    Backup breach on the CCTV wall to Cash. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Cash

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the CCTV wall to Cash. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Cash

    EMP the CCTV wall to Cash right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Weapons

    Lance the denial on the CCTV wall to Cash from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Vault Checkpoint.

  6. AS

    AshEntry · Weapons

    Take Weapons first and clear the doorway into CCTV for the plant.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Screens

    Drive your shock drone into Vault Checkpoint and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Break

    Airjabs at Break so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setupB

Where each defender goes on CCTV / Vault Checkpoint

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · CCTV

    Claw the CCTV wall to Cash from inside CCTV so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Cash outside wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · CCTV

    Sit on the CCTV wall to Cash with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · CCTV

    Jammer by the Weapons door into CCTV. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Vault Checkpoint

    Mirror the wall between Vault Checkpoint and CCTV, so you can watch the plant in CCTV from Vault Checkpoint without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · CCTV

    ADS on the Screens side of CCTV. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Weapons

    Cams in Weapons and Screens, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Vault Checkpoint

    Anchor Vault Checkpoint. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. VI

    VigilRoam · Break

    Roam Break and the rooms around it. Take one fight, then fall back to Vault Checkpoint by 1:00.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the CCTV wall to Cash from Cash.
  • Take Weapons early and drone the site.
  • Clear Break so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the CCTV wall to Cash stays denied, push Weapons and Screens together with smokes and plant in Vault Checkpoint.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the CCTV wall to Cash first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Cash outside wall next.
  • Watch Weapons and Screens. That is where the inside push comes from.

If it goes wrong

If the CCTV wall to Cash opens early, fall back into Vault Checkpoint and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
