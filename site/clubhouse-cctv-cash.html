AttackSolo2F · CCTV / CashDraft · Y11S3

Bring your own breach or your own anti-denial.

The plan · numbers match the blueprint

  1. Ace or Maverick opens the CCTV outside wall even with no help.
  2. Drone Construction for your randoms and call what you see.

  • Maverick

  • Kali

Deny the CCTV outside wall yourself and play from Cash.

  2. Reinforce the CCTV outside wall before anything else.
  3. Call "Construction" the moment you see a drone there.

Attack basics, any squad size

  • Hard breach the CCTV outside wall from 752.
  • Take Construction early and drone the site.
  • Clear Red stairs so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the CCTV outside wall stays denied, push Construction and Garage rafters together with smokes and plant in Cash.

What it looks like in game

Defence basics, any squad size

  • Reinforce the CCTV outside wall first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Cash wall to Construction next.
  • Watch Construction and Garage rafters. That is where the inside push comes from.

If it goes wrong

If the CCTV outside wall opens early, fall back into Cash and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
