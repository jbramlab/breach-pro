AttackOperator setupB

Where each attacker goes on Lab Support / Lab

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Lockers

    Breach the Lab Support wall to Lockers from Lockers. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Lockers

    Backup breach on the Lab Support wall to Lockers. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Lockers

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Lab Support wall to Lockers. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Lockers

    EMP the Lab Support wall to Lockers right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Missile Hallway

    Lance the denial on the Lab Support wall to Lockers from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Lab.

  6. BU

    BuckVertical · 1F above

    Play Armory above Lab. Open small holes over the default anchor spots in Lab.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Lab Exit

    Drive your shock drone into Lab and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Lockers

    Airjabs at Lockers so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setupB

Where each defender goes on Lab Support / Lab

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Lab Support

    Claw the Lab Support wall to Lockers from inside Lab Support so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Lab Support / Lab wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Lab Support

    Sit on the Lab Support wall to Lockers with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Lab Support

    Jammer by the Missile Hallway door into Lab Support. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Lab

    Mirror the wall between Lab and Lab Support, so you can watch the plant in Lab Support from Lab without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Lab Support

    ADS on the Lab Exit side of Lab Support. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Missile Hallway

    Cams in Missile Hallway and Lab Exit, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Lab

    Anchor Lab. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. PU

    PulseVertical hunt · 1F above

    Go up to Armory above Lab early. Pulse attackers setting up vertical and pick them off before they open the floor.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Lab Support wall to Lockers from Lockers.
  • Play vertical from Armory above Lab to pin the anchors.
  • Clear Lockers so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Lab Support wall to Lockers stays denied, push Missile Hallway and Lab Exit together with smokes and plant in Lab.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Lab Support wall to Lockers first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Lab Support / Lab wall next.
  • Watch Armory above Lab. Attackers will play vertical from there.

If it goes wrong

If the Lab Support wall to Lockers opens early, fall back into Lab and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
