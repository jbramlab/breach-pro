AttackOperator setup1F

Where each attacker goes on Lounge / Bar

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Pool

    Breach the Bar wall to Pool from Pool. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Pool

    Backup breach on the Bar wall to Pool. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Pool

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Bar wall to Pool. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Pool

    EMP the Bar wall to Pool right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Lobby

    Lance the denial on the Bar wall to Pool from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Lounge.

  6. BU

    BuckVertical · 2F above

    Play Music above Bar. Open small holes over the default anchor spots in Bar.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Ninety

    Drive your shock drone into Lounge and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Toilets

    Airjabs at Toilets so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup1F

Where each defender goes on Lounge / Bar

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Bar

    Claw the Bar wall to Pool from inside Bar so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Ninety / Lounge wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Bar

    Sit on the Bar wall to Pool with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Lounge

    Jammer by the Lobby door into Lounge. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Lounge

    Mirror the wall between Lounge and Bar, so you can watch the plant in Bar from Lounge without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Lounge

    ADS on the Ninety side of Lounge. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Lobby

    Cams in Lobby and Ninety, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Lounge

    Anchor Lounge. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. PU

    PulseVertical hunt · 2F above

    Go up to Music above Bar early. Pulse attackers setting up vertical and pick them off before they open the floor.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Bar wall to Pool from Pool.
  • Play vertical from Music above Bar to pin the anchors.
  • Clear Toilets so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Bar wall to Pool stays denied, push Lobby and Ninety together with smokes and plant in Bar.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Bar wall to Pool first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Ninety / Lounge wall next.
  • Watch Music above Bar. Attackers will play vertical from there.

If it goes wrong

If the Bar wall to Pool opens early, fall back into Bar and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
