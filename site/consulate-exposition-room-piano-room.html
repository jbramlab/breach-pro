You plus four randoms

  2. Drone Piano Hallway for your randoms and call what you see.

AttackOperator setup1F

Where each attacker goes on Exposition Room / Piano Room

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Lobby

    Breach the Exposition Room wall to Lobby from Lobby. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Lobby

    Backup breach on the Exposition Room wall to Lobby. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Lobby

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Exposition Room wall to Lobby. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Lobby

    EMP the Exposition Room wall to Lobby right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Piano Hallway

    Lance the denial on the Exposition Room wall to Lobby from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Piano Room.

  6. BU

    BuckVertical · 2F above

    Play Meeting Room above Exposition Room. Open small holes over the default anchor spots in Exposition Room.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Closet

    Drive your shock drone into Piano Room and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Yellow Stairs

    Airjabs at Yellow Stairs so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup1F

Where each defender goes on Exposition Room / Piano Room

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Exposition Room

    Claw the Exposition Room wall to Lobby from inside Exposition Room so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Tellers / Lounge wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Exposition Room

    Sit on the Exposition Room wall to Lobby with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Piano Room

    Jammer by the Piano Hallway door into Piano Room. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Piano Room

    Mirror the wall between Piano Room and Exposition Room, so you can watch the plant in Exposition Room from Piano Room without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Piano Room

    ADS on the Closet side of Piano Room. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Piano Hallway

    Cams in Piano Hallway and Closet, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Piano Room

    Anchor Piano Room. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. PU

    PulseVertical hunt · 2F above

    Go up to Meeting Room above Exposition Room early. Pulse attackers setting up vertical and pick them off before they open the floor.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Exposition Room wall to Lobby from Lobby.
  • Play vertical from Meeting Room above Exposition Room to pin the anchors.
  • Clear Yellow Stairs so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Exposition Room wall to Lobby stays denied, push Piano Hallway and Closet together with smokes and plant in Piano Room.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Exposition Room wall to Lobby first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Tellers / Lounge wall next.
  • Watch Meeting Room above Exposition Room. Attackers will play vertical from there.

If it goes wrong

If the Exposition Room wall to Lobby opens early, fall back into Piano Room and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
