Attack basics, any squad size

  • Hard breach the CEO east wall from Skylight stairwell.
  • Take Janitor early and drone the site.
  • Clear Main stairs so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the CEO east wall stays denied, push Janitor and Conference together with smokes and plant in Executive lounge.
