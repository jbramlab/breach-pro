Attack basics, any squad size

  • Hard breach the Archives wall to Skylight stairwell from Skylight stairwell.
  • Take Tellers early and drone the site.
  • Clear Electrical so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Archives wall to Skylight stairwell stays denied, push Tellers and Staff room together with smokes and plant in Tellers' office.

What it looks like in game

Defence basics, any squad size

  • Reinforce the Archives wall to Skylight stairwell first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Tellers' office wall to Tellers next.
  • Watch Tellers and Staff room. That is where the inside push comes from.

If it goes wrong

If the Archives wall to Skylight stairwell opens early, fall back into Tellers' office and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
