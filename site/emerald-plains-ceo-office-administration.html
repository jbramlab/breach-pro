CEO Office / Administration

Best guess The two bomb rooms on this plan are our best guess from the blueprint. Check it in a custom game before you rely on it.

You plus four randoms

Bring your own breach or your own anti-denial.

The plan

  2. Drone Folding Screen for your randoms and call what you see.

  • Maverick

  • Kali

AttackOperator setup2F

Where each attacker goes on CEO Office / Administration

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Meeting

    Breach the CEO Office wall to Meeting from Meeting. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Meeting

    Backup breach on the CEO Office wall to Meeting. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Meeting

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the CEO Office wall to Meeting. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Meeting

    EMP the CEO Office wall to Meeting right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Folding Screen

    Lance the denial on the CEO Office wall to Meeting from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Administration.

  6. AS

    AshEntry · Folding Screen

    Take Folding Screen first and clear the doorway into CEO Office for the plant.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Waiting

    Drive your shock drone into Administration and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Green Stairs

    Airjabs at Green Stairs so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup2F

Where each defender goes on CEO Office / Administration

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · CEO Office

    Claw the CEO Office wall to Meeting from inside CEO Office so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Painting / Meeting wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · CEO Office

    Sit on the CEO Office wall to Meeting with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · CEO Office

    Jammer by the Folding Screen door into CEO Office. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Administration

    Mirror the wall between Administration and CEO Office, so you can watch the plant in CEO Office from Administration without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Administration

    ADS on the Waiting side of Administration. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Folding Screen

    Cams in Folding Screen and Waiting, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Administration

    Anchor Administration. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. VI

    VigilRoam · Green Stairs

    Roam Green Stairs and the rooms around it. Take one fight, then fall back to Administration by 1:00.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the CEO Office wall to Meeting from Meeting.
  • Take Folding Screen early and drone the site.
  • Clear Green Stairs so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the CEO Office wall to Meeting stays denied, push Folding Screen and Waiting together with smokes and plant in Administration.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the CEO Office wall to Meeting first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Painting / Meeting wall next.
  • Watch Folding Screen and Waiting. That is where the inside push comes from.

If it goes wrong

If the CEO Office wall to Meeting opens early, fall back into Administration and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
