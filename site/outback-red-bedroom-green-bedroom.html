Red Bedroom / Green Bedroom

You plus four randoms
AttackSolo1FDraft · Y11S3

Play vertical yourself. It works without team help.

The plan

  1. Get to Piano Room above Green Bedroom and open holes over common anchor spots.
  2. Drone Bathroom for your randoms and call what you see.

  • Sledge
  • Ace
  • Zofia

AttackOperator setup1F

Where each attacker goes on Red Bedroom / Green Bedroom

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Yellow Stairs

    Breach the Red Bedroom wall to Yellow Stairs from Yellow Stairs. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Yellow Stairs

    Backup breach on the Red Bedroom wall to Yellow Stairs. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Yellow Stairs

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Red Bedroom wall to Yellow Stairs. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Yellow Stairs

    EMP the Red Bedroom wall to Yellow Stairs right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Bathroom

    Lance the denial on the Red Bedroom wall to Yellow Stairs from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Green Bedroom.

  6. BU

    BuckVertical · 2F above

    Play Piano Room above Green Bedroom. Open small holes over the default anchor spots in Green Bedroom.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Reptile

    Drive your shock drone into Green Bedroom and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Closet

    Airjabs at Closet so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup1F

Where each defender goes on Red Bedroom / Green Bedroom

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Red Bedroom

    Claw the Red Bedroom wall to Yellow Stairs from inside Red Bedroom so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Closet / Reptile wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Red Bedroom

    Sit on the Red Bedroom wall to Yellow Stairs with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Red Bedroom

    Jammer by the Bathroom door into Red Bedroom. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Green Bedroom

    Mirror the wall between Green Bedroom and Red Bedroom, so you can watch the plant in Red Bedroom from Green Bedroom without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Red Bedroom

    ADS on the Reptile side of Red Bedroom. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Bathroom

    Cams in Bathroom and Reptile, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Green Bedroom

    Anchor Green Bedroom. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. PU

    PulseVertical hunt · 2F above

    Go up to Piano Room above Green Bedroom early. Pulse attackers setting up vertical and pick them off before they open the floor.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Red Bedroom wall to Yellow Stairs from Yellow Stairs.
  • Play vertical from Piano Room above Green Bedroom to pin the anchors.
  • Clear Closet so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Red Bedroom wall to Yellow Stairs stays denied, push Bathroom and Reptile together with smokes and plant in Green Bedroom.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Red Bedroom wall to Yellow Stairs first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Closet / Reptile wall next.
  • Watch Piano Room above Green Bedroom. Attackers will play vertical from there.

If it goes wrong

If the Red Bedroom wall to Yellow Stairs opens early, fall back into Green Bedroom and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
