AttackOperator setup1F

Where each attacker goes on Dining / Kitchen

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Ninety

    Breach the Dining wall to Ninety from Ninety. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Ninety

    Backup breach on the Dining wall to Ninety. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Ninety

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Dining wall to Ninety. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Ninety

    EMP the Dining wall to Ninety right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Trash

    Lance the denial on the Dining wall to Ninety from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Kitchen.

  6. BU

    BuckVertical · 2F above

    Play Archives above Kitchen. Open small holes over the default anchor spots in Kitchen.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Cross

    Drive your shock drone into Kitchen and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Orange Stairs

    Airjabs at Orange Stairs so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup1F

Where each defender goes on Dining / Kitchen

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Dining

    Claw the Dining wall to Ninety from inside Dining so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Cross / Dining wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Dining

    Sit on the Dining wall to Ninety with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Kitchen

    Jammer by the Trash door into Kitchen. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Kitchen

    Mirror the wall between Kitchen and Dining, so you can watch the plant in Dining from Kitchen without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Dining

    ADS on the Cross side of Dining. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Trash

    Cams in Trash and Cross, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Kitchen

    Anchor Kitchen. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. PU

    PulseVertical hunt · 2F above

    Go up to Archives above Kitchen early. Pulse attackers setting up vertical and pick them off before they open the floor.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Dining wall to Ninety from Ninety.
  • Play vertical from Archives above Kitchen to pin the anchors.
  • Clear Orange Stairs so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Dining wall to Ninety stays denied, push Trash and Cross together with smokes and plant in Kitchen.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Dining wall to Ninety first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Cross / Dining wall next.
  • Watch Archives above Kitchen. Attackers will play vertical from there.

If it goes wrong

If the Dining wall to Ninety opens early, fall back into Kitchen and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
