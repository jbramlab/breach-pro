AttackOperator setup1F

Where each attacker goes on Bar / Stage

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Kitchen Hallway

    Breach the Stage wall to Kitchen Hallway from Kitchen Hallway. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Kitchen Hallway

    Backup breach on the Stage wall to Kitchen Hallway. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Kitchen Hallway

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Stage wall to Kitchen Hallway. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Kitchen Hallway

    EMP the Stage wall to Kitchen Hallway right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Lobby

    Lance the denial on the Stage wall to Kitchen Hallway from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Bar.

  6. BU

    BuckVertical · 2F above

    Play Construction above Stage. Open small holes over the default anchor spots in Stage.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Toilets

    Drive your shock drone into Bar and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Main Stairs

    Airjabs at Main Stairs so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup1F

Where each defender goes on Bar / Stage

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Stage

    Claw the Stage wall to Kitchen Hallway from inside Stage so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Pool / side room wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Stage

    Sit on the Stage wall to Kitchen Hallway with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Stage

    Jammer by the Lobby door into Stage. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Bar

    Mirror the wall between Bar and Stage, so you can watch the plant in Stage from Bar without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Bar

    ADS on the Toilets side of Bar. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Lobby

    Cams in Lobby and Toilets, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Bar

    Anchor Bar. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. PU

    PulseVertical hunt · 2F above

    Go up to Construction above Stage early. Pulse attackers setting up vertical and pick them off before they open the floor.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Stage wall to Kitchen Hallway from Kitchen Hallway.
  • Play vertical from Construction above Stage to pin the anchors.
  • Clear Main Stairs so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Stage wall to Kitchen Hallway stays denied, push Lobby and Toilets together with smokes and plant in Stage.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Stage wall to Kitchen Hallway first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Pool / side room wall next.
  • Watch Construction above Stage. Attackers will play vertical from there.

If it goes wrong

If the Stage wall to Kitchen Hallway opens early, fall back into Stage and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
