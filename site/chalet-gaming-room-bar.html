AttackOperator setup1F

Where each attacker goes on Gaming Room / Bar

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Bar Stock

    Breach the Gaming Room wall to Bar Stock from Bar Stock. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Bar Stock

    Backup breach on the Gaming Room wall to Bar Stock. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Bar Stock

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Gaming Room wall to Bar Stock. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Bar Stock

    EMP the Gaming Room wall to Bar Stock right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Gaming Hallway

    Lance the denial on the Gaming Room wall to Bar Stock from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Bar.

  6. BU

    BuckVertical · 2F above

    Play Mezzanine above Bar. Open small holes over the default anchor spots in Bar.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Library Stairs

    Drive your shock drone into Bar and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Bar Hallway

    Airjabs at Bar Hallway so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup1F

Where each defender goes on Gaming Room / Bar

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Gaming Room

    Claw the Gaming Room wall to Bar Stock from inside Gaming Room so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Dining Hallway / Dining wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Gaming Room

    Sit on the Gaming Room wall to Bar Stock with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Bar

    Jammer by the Gaming Hallway door into Bar. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Bar

    Mirror the wall between Bar and Gaming Room, so you can watch the plant in Gaming Room from Bar without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Gaming Room

    ADS on the Library Stairs side of Gaming Room. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Gaming Hallway

    Cams in Gaming Hallway and Library Stairs, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Bar

    Anchor Bar. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. PU

    PulseVertical hunt · 2F above

    Go up to Mezzanine above Bar early. Pulse attackers setting up vertical and pick them off before they open the floor.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Gaming Room wall to Bar Stock from Bar Stock.
  • Play vertical from Mezzanine above Bar to pin the anchors.
  • Clear Bar Hallway so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Gaming Room wall to Bar Stock stays denied, push Gaming Hallway and Library Stairs together with smokes and plant in Bar.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Gaming Room wall to Bar Stock first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Dining Hallway / Dining wall next.
  • Watch Mezzanine above Bar. Attackers will play vertical from there.

If it goes wrong

If the Gaming Room wall to Bar Stock opens early, fall back into Bar and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
