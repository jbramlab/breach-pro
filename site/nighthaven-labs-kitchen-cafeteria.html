Kitchen / Cafeteria

Best guess The two bomb rooms on this plan are our best guess from the blueprint. Check it in a custom game before you rely on it.

You plus four randoms
AttackSolo1FDraft · Y11S3

Bring your own breach or your own anti-denial.

The plan

  1. Ace or Maverick opens the Kitchen outside wall even with no help.
  2. Drone Lobby Stairs for your randoms and call what you see.

Operators · first pick is you

  • ACAce
  • Maverick
  • Thatcher
  • Kali

  • THThatcher

  • Kali

AttackOperator setup1F

Where each attacker goes on Kitchen / Cafeteria

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Outside

    Breach the Kitchen outside wall from Outside. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Outside

    Backup breach on the Kitchen outside wall. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Outside

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Kitchen outside wall. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Outside

    EMP the Kitchen outside wall right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Lobby Stairs

    Lance the denial on the Kitchen outside wall from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Cafeteria.

  6. AS

    AshEntry · Lobby Stairs

    Take Lobby Stairs first and clear the doorway into Cafeteria for the plant.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Lobby Hallway

    Drive your shock drone into Cafeteria and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Lobby

    Airjabs at Lobby so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup1F

Where each defender goes on Kitchen / Cafeteria

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Kitchen

    Claw the Kitchen outside wall from inside Kitchen so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Cafeteria / Kitchen wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Kitchen

    Sit on the Kitchen outside wall with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Cafeteria

    Jammer by the Lobby Stairs door into Cafeteria. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Cafeteria

    Mirror the wall between Cafeteria and Kitchen, so you can watch the plant in Kitchen from Cafeteria without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Cafeteria

    ADS on the Lobby Hallway side of Cafeteria. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Lobby Stairs

    Cams in Lobby Stairs and Lobby Hallway, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Cafeteria

    Anchor Cafeteria. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. VI

    VigilRoam · Lobby

    Roam Lobby and the rooms around it. Take one fight, then fall back to Cafeteria by 1:00.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Kitchen outside wall from Outside.
  • Take Lobby Stairs early and drone the site.
  • Clear Lobby so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Kitchen outside wall stays denied, push Lobby Stairs and Lobby Hallway together with smokes and plant in Cafeteria.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Kitchen outside wall first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Cafeteria / Kitchen wall next.
  • Watch Lobby Stairs and Lobby Hallway. That is where the inside push comes from.

If it goes wrong

If the Kitchen outside wall opens early, fall back into Cafeteria and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
