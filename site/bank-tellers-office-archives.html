  3. Call "Lobby" the moment you see a drone there.

AttackOperator setup1F

Where each attacker goes on Tellers' Office / Archives

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Open Space Hallway

    Breach the Tellers' Office wall to Open Space Hallway from Open Space Hallway. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Open Space Hallway

    Backup breach on the Tellers' Office wall to Open Space Hallway. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Open Space Hallway

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Tellers' Office wall to Open Space Hallway. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Open Space Hallway

    EMP the Tellers' Office wall to Open Space Hallway right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Lobby

    Lance the denial on the Tellers' Office wall to Open Space Hallway from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Archives.

  6. BU

    BuckVertical · 2F above

    Play CEO Office above Archives. Open small holes over the default anchor spots in Archives.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Square Stairs

    Drive your shock drone into Archives and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Lobby Stairs

    Airjabs at Lobby Stairs so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup1F

Where each defender goes on Tellers' Office / Archives

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Tellers' Office

    Claw the Tellers' Office wall to Open Space Hallway from inside Tellers' Office so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Staff Room / side room wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Tellers' Office

    Sit on the Tellers' Office wall to Open Space Hallway with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Tellers' Office

    Jammer by the Lobby door into Tellers' Office. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Archives

    Mirror the wall between Archives and Tellers' Office, so you can watch the plant in Tellers' Office from Archives without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Archives

    ADS on the Square Stairs side of Archives. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Lobby

    Cams in Lobby and Square Stairs, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Archives

    Anchor Archives. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. PU

    PulseVertical hunt · 2F above

    Go up to CEO Office above Archives early. Pulse attackers setting up vertical and pick them off before they open the floor.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Tellers' Office wall to Open Space Hallway from Open Space Hallway.
  • Play vertical from CEO Office above Archives to pin the anchors.
  • Clear Lobby Stairs so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Tellers' Office wall to Open Space Hallway stays denied, push Lobby and Square Stairs together with smokes and plant in Archives.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Tellers' Office wall to Open Space Hallway first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Staff Room / side room wall next.
  • Watch CEO Office above Archives. Attackers will play vertical from there.

If it goes wrong

If the Tellers' Office wall to Open Space Hallway opens early, fall back into Archives and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
