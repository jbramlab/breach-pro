AttackOperator setup1F

Where each attacker goes on Bathroom / Tellers

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Waiting

    Breach the Tellers wall to Waiting from Waiting. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Waiting

    Backup breach on the Tellers wall to Waiting. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Waiting

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Tellers wall to Waiting. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Waiting

    EMP the Tellers wall to Waiting right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Bathroom Hallway

    Lance the denial on the Tellers wall to Waiting from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Bathroom.

  6. BU

    BuckVertical · 2F above

    Play Offices above Tellers. Open small holes over the default anchor spots in Tellers.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Lobby

    Drive your shock drone into Bathroom and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Main Stairs

    Airjabs at Main Stairs so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup1F

Where each defender goes on Bathroom / Tellers

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Tellers

    Claw the Tellers wall to Waiting from inside Tellers so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Passport / Waiting wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Tellers

    Sit on the Tellers wall to Waiting with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Bathroom

    Jammer by the Bathroom Hallway door into Bathroom. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Bathroom

    Mirror the wall between Bathroom and Tellers, so you can watch the plant in Tellers from Bathroom without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Tellers

    ADS on the Lobby side of Tellers. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Bathroom Hallway

    Cams in Bathroom Hallway and Lobby, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Bathroom

    Anchor Bathroom. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. PU

    PulseVertical hunt · 2F above

    Go up to Offices above Tellers early. Pulse attackers setting up vertical and pick them off before they open the floor.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Tellers wall to Waiting from Waiting.
  • Play vertical from Offices above Tellers to pin the anchors.
  • Clear Main Stairs so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Tellers wall to Waiting stays denied, push Bathroom Hallway and Lobby together with smokes and plant in Bathroom.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Tellers wall to Waiting first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Passport / Waiting wall next.
  • Watch Offices above Tellers. Attackers will play vertical from there.

If it goes wrong

If the Tellers wall to Waiting opens early, fall back into Bathroom and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
