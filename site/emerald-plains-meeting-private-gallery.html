You plus four randoms

Bring your own breach or your own anti-denial.

  • Maverick
  • Thatcher
  • Kali

AttackOperator setup2F

Where each attacker goes on Meeting / Private Gallery

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Painting

    Breach the Meeting wall to Painting from Painting. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Painting

    Backup breach on the Meeting wall to Painting. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Painting

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Meeting wall to Painting. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Painting

    EMP the Meeting wall to Painting right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Clock Hallway

    Lance the denial on the Meeting wall to Painting from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Private Gallery.

  6. AS

    AshEntry · Clock Hallway

    Take Clock Hallway first and clear the doorway into Private Gallery for the plant.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Folding Screen

    Drive your shock drone into Private Gallery and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Orange Stairs

    Airjabs at Orange Stairs so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup2F

Where each defender goes on Meeting / Private Gallery

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Meeting

    Claw the Meeting wall to Painting from inside Meeting so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Private Gallery / Ceramic wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Meeting

    Sit on the Meeting wall to Painting with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Private Gallery

    Jammer by the Clock Hallway door into Private Gallery. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Private Gallery

    Mirror the wall between Private Gallery and Meeting, so you can watch the plant in Meeting from Private Gallery without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Meeting

    ADS on the Folding Screen side of Meeting. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Clock Hallway

    Cams in Clock Hallway and Folding Screen, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Private Gallery

    Anchor Private Gallery. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. VI

    VigilRoam · Orange Stairs

    Roam Orange Stairs and the rooms around it. Take one fight, then fall back to Private Gallery by 1:00.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Meeting wall to Painting from Painting.
  • Take Clock Hallway early and drone the site.
  • Clear Orange Stairs so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Meeting wall to Painting stays denied, push Clock Hallway and Folding Screen together with smokes and plant in Private Gallery.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Meeting wall to Painting first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Private Gallery / Ceramic wall next.
  • Watch Clock Hallway and Folding Screen. That is where the inside push comes from.

If it goes wrong

If the Meeting wall to Painting opens early, fall back into Private Gallery and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
