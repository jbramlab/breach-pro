AttackOperator setup1F

Where each attacker goes on Armory / Weapon Maintenance

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Medical

    Breach the Armory wall to Medical from Medical. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Medical

    Backup breach on the Armory wall to Medical. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Medical

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Armory wall to Medical. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Medical

    EMP the Armory wall to Medical right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Blue Hallway

    Lance the denial on the Armory wall to Medical from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Weapon Maintenance.

  6. BU

    BuckVertical · 2F above

    Play Operational above Weapon Maintenance. Open small holes over the default anchor spots in Weapon Maintenance.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Staff Stairs

    Drive your shock drone into Weapon Maintenance and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Shooting Stairs

    Airjabs at Shooting Stairs so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup1F

Where each defender goes on Armory / Weapon Maintenance

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Armory

    Claw the Armory wall to Medical from inside Armory so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Armory / Blue Hallway wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Armory

    Sit on the Armory wall to Medical with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Armory

    Jammer by the Blue Hallway door into Armory. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Weapon Maintenance

    Mirror the wall between Weapon Maintenance and Armory, so you can watch the plant in Armory from Weapon Maintenance without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Weapon Maintenance

    ADS on the Staff Stairs side of Weapon Maintenance. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Blue Hallway

    Cams in Blue Hallway and Staff Stairs, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Weapon Maintenance

    Anchor Weapon Maintenance. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. PU

    PulseVertical hunt · 2F above

    Go up to Operational above Weapon Maintenance early. Pulse attackers setting up vertical and pick them off before they open the floor.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Armory wall to Medical from Medical.
  • Play vertical from Operational above Weapon Maintenance to pin the anchors.
  • Clear Shooting Stairs so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Armory wall to Medical stays denied, push Blue Hallway and Staff Stairs together with smokes and plant in Weapon Maintenance.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Armory wall to Medical first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Armory / Blue Hallway wall next.
  • Watch Operational above Weapon Maintenance. Attackers will play vertical from there.

If it goes wrong

If the Armory wall to Medical opens early, fall back into Weapon Maintenance and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
