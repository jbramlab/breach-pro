Office / Master Bedroom

You plus four randoms
AttackSolo2FDraft · Y11S3

Bring your own breach or your own anti-denial.

The plan

  1. Ace or Maverick opens the Office wall to Mezzanine even with no help.
  2. Drone Library Hallway for your randoms and call what you see.

Operators · first pick is you

  • ACAce
  • Maverick
  • Thatcher
  • Kali

Common mistake

Breaching without droning, so the denier just stays alive.

AttackDuo2FDraft · Y11S3

Breacher plus support on the wall.

The plan

  1. Thatcher drones the wall, then EMPs the denier.
  2. Thermite opens the Office wall to Mezzanine and you both trade into Office.

Operators · first 2 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • Hibana
  • Kali

Common mistake

Support forgetting to drone again after the EMP.

AttackTrio2FDraft · Y11S3

Two on the wall, one pushing Library Hallway.

The plan

  2. Open the Office wall to Mezzanine while they are looking at Ash.

Operators · first 3 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • ASAsh

Wall team, inside push and a flank watch.

  3. Plant in Office with the hole covering you.

The plan

  2. Thatcher EMPs and Thermite opens the Office wall to Mezzanine at about 1:30.
  3. Ash and Ying hit Library Hallway and Office Balcony together. Nomad holds Library Stairs.

Operators · first 5 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • ASAsh

  • NONomad

DefenceSolo2FDraft · Y11S3

Deny the Office wall to Mezzanine yourself and play from Master Bedroom.

The plan

  1. Put your denial on the Office wall to Mezzanine so it survives even if your randoms do nothing.
  2. Reinforce the Office wall to Mezzanine before anything else.
  3. Call "Library Hallway" the moment you see a drone there.

Operators · first pick is you

  • KDKaid
  • Mute
  • Bandit
  • Mira

Common mistake

Roaming as a solo. Randoms rarely hold site while you are away.

  2. Valkyrie cams cover Library Hallway and Office Balcony so you both see pushes early.
  3. Swap between Office and Master Bedroom when one side gets hit, and call it before you move.

AttackOperator setup2F

Where each attacker goes on Office / Master Bedroom

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Mezzanine

    Breach the Office wall to Mezzanine from Mezzanine. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Mezzanine

    Backup breach on the Office wall to Mezzanine. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Mezzanine

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Office wall to Mezzanine. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Mezzanine

    EMP the Office wall to Mezzanine right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Library Hallway

    Lance the denial on the Office wall to Mezzanine from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Master Bedroom.

  6. AS

    AshEntry · Library Hallway

    Take Library Hallway first and clear the doorway into Office for the plant.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Office Balcony

    Drive your shock drone into Master Bedroom and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Library Stairs

    Airjabs at Library Stairs so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup2F

Where each defender goes on Office / Master Bedroom

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Office

    Claw the Office wall to Mezzanine from inside Office so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Mezzanine / Library wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Office

    Sit on the Office wall to Mezzanine with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Office

    Jammer by the Library Hallway door into Office. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Master Bedroom

    Mirror the wall between Master Bedroom and Office, so you can watch the plant in Office from Master Bedroom without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Office

    ADS on the Office Balcony side of Office. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Library Hallway

    Cams in Library Hallway and Office Balcony, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Master Bedroom

    Anchor Master Bedroom. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. VI

    VigilRoam · Library Stairs

    Roam Library Stairs and the rooms around it. Take one fight, then fall back to Master Bedroom by 1:00.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Office wall to Mezzanine from Mezzanine.
  • Take Library Hallway early and drone the site.
  • Clear Library Stairs so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Office wall to Mezzanine stays denied, push Library Hallway and Office Balcony together with smokes and plant in Master Bedroom.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Office wall to Mezzanine first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Mezzanine / Library wall next.
  • Watch Library Hallway and Office Balcony. That is where the inside push comes from.

If it goes wrong

If the Office wall to Mezzanine opens early, fall back into Master Bedroom and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
