Laundry / Piano Room

You plus four randoms
AttackSolo2FDraft · Y11S3

Bring your own breach or your own anti-denial.

The plan

  1. Ace or Maverick opens the Piano Room wall to Gaming even with no help.
  2. Drone Connector for your randoms and call what you see.

Operators · first pick is you

  • ACAce
  • Maverick
  • Thatcher
  • Kali

Common mistake

Breaching without droning, so the denier just stays alive.

AttackDuo2FDraft · Y11S3

Breacher plus support on the wall.

The plan

  2. Thermite opens the Piano Room wall to Gaming and you both trade into Piano Room.

Operators · first 2 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • Hibana
  • Kali

Common mistake

Support forgetting to drone again after the EMP.

AttackTrio2FDraft · Y11S3

Two on the wall, one pushing Connector.

The plan

  2. Open the Piano Room wall to Gaming while they are looking at Ash.

Operators · first 3 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • ASAsh

Wall team, inside push and a flank watch.

  2. Ace and Kali open the Piano Room wall to Gaming as Ash pushes Connector.
  3. Plant in Piano Room with the hole covering you.

Operators · first 4 are your squad

  • KAKali

Attack5-stack2FDraft · Y11S3

Full execute: wall, two pushes and a flank watch.

The plan

  1. 0:00 to 1:00: drone in and take Connector.
  2. Thatcher EMPs and Thermite opens the Piano Room wall to Gaming at about 1:30.
  3. Ash and Ying hit Connector and Motel Hallway together. Nomad holds Yellow Stairs.

Operators · first 5 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • ASAsh
  • YIYing
  • NONomad

Common mistake

DefenceSolo2FDraft · Y11S3

Deny the Piano Room wall to Gaming yourself and play from Laundry.

The plan

  1. Put your denial on the Piano Room wall to Gaming so it survives even if your randoms do nothing.
  2. Reinforce the Piano Room wall to Gaming before anything else.
  3. Call "Connector" the moment you see a drone there.

Operators · first pick is you

  • KDKaid
  • Mute
  • Bandit
  • Mira

Common mistake

Roaming as a solo. Randoms rarely hold site while you are away.

DefenceDuo2FDraft · Y11S3

One anchor per room, one of you on cams.

The plan

  1. Kaid holds Piano Room and keeps the Piano Room wall to Gaming electrified.
  2. Valkyrie cams cover Connector and Motel Hallway so you both see pushes early.
  3. Swap between Laundry and Piano Room when one side gets hit, and call it before you move.

  • Bandit
  • Echo

AttackOperator setup2F

Where each attacker goes on Laundry / Piano Room

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Gaming

    Breach the Piano Room wall to Gaming from Gaming. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Gaming

    Backup breach on the Piano Room wall to Gaming. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Gaming

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Piano Room wall to Gaming. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Gaming

    EMP the Piano Room wall to Gaming right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Connector

    Lance the denial on the Piano Room wall to Gaming from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Laundry.

  6. AS

    AshEntry · Connector

    Take Connector first and clear the doorway into Piano Room for the plant.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Motel Hallway

    Drive your shock drone into Laundry and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Yellow Stairs

    Airjabs at Yellow Stairs so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup2F

Where each defender goes on Laundry / Piano Room

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Piano Room

    Claw the Piano Room wall to Gaming from inside Piano Room so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Terrace / Gaming wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Piano Room

    Sit on the Piano Room wall to Gaming with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Piano Room

    Jammer by the Connector door into Piano Room. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Laundry

    Mirror the wall between Laundry and Piano Room, so you can watch the plant in Piano Room from Laundry without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Laundry

    ADS on the Motel Hallway side of Laundry. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Connector

    Cams in Connector and Motel Hallway, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Laundry

    Anchor Laundry. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. VI

    VigilRoam · Yellow Stairs

    Roam Yellow Stairs and the rooms around it. Take one fight, then fall back to Laundry by 1:00.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Piano Room wall to Gaming from Gaming.
  • Take Connector early and drone the site.
  • Clear Yellow Stairs so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Piano Room wall to Gaming stays denied, push Connector and Motel Hallway together with smokes and plant in Laundry.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Piano Room wall to Gaming first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Terrace / Gaming wall next.
  • Watch Connector and Motel Hallway. That is where the inside push comes from.

If it goes wrong

If the Piano Room wall to Gaming opens early, fall back into Laundry and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
