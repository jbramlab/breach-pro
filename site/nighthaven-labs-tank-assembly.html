AttackOperator setupB

Where each attacker goes on Tank / Assembly

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Animus

    Breach the Tank wall to Animus from Animus. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Animus

    Backup breach on the Tank wall to Animus. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Animus

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Tank wall to Animus. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Animus

    EMP the Tank wall to Animus right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Nano Hallway

    Lance the denial on the Tank wall to Animus from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Assembly.

  6. BU

    BuckVertical · 1F above

    Play Delivery above Assembly. Open small holes over the default anchor spots in Assembly.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Animus Delivery

    Drive your shock drone into Assembly and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Cargo Stairs

    Airjabs at Cargo Stairs so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setupB

Where each defender goes on Tank / Assembly

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Tank

    Claw the Tank wall to Animus from inside Tank so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the side room / Exo Hallway wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Tank

    Sit on the Tank wall to Animus with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Tank

    Jammer by the Nano Hallway door into Tank. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Assembly

    Mirror the wall between Assembly and Tank, so you can watch the plant in Tank from Assembly without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Tank

    ADS on the Animus Delivery side of Tank. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Nano Hallway

    Cams in Nano Hallway and Animus Delivery, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Assembly

    Anchor Assembly. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. PU

    PulseVertical hunt · 1F above

    Go up to Delivery above Assembly early. Pulse attackers setting up vertical and pick them off before they open the floor.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Tank wall to Animus from Animus.
  • Play vertical from Delivery above Assembly to pin the anchors.
  • Clear Cargo Stairs so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Tank wall to Animus stays denied, push Nano Hallway and Animus Delivery together with smokes and plant in Assembly.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Tank wall to Animus first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the side room / Exo Hallway wall next.
  • Watch Delivery above Assembly. Attackers will play vertical from there.

If it goes wrong

If the Tank wall to Animus opens early, fall back into Assembly and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
