Consul Office / Meeting Room

You plus four randoms
AttackSolo2FDraft · Y11S3

Bring your own breach or your own anti-denial.

The plan

  1. Ace or Maverick opens the Consul Office outside wall even with no help.
  2. Drone Break Hallway for your randoms and call what you see.

Operators · first pick is you

  • ACAce
  • Maverick
  • Thatcher
  • Kali

Common mistake

Breaching without droning, so the denier just stays alive.

AttackDuo2FDraft · Y11S3

Breacher plus support on the wall.

The plan

  1. Thatcher drones the wall, then EMPs the denier.
  2. Thermite opens the Consul Office outside wall and you both trade into Consul Office.

Operators · first 2 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • Hibana
  • Kali

Common mistake

Support forgetting to drone again after the EMP.

AttackTrio2FDraft · Y11S3

Two on the wall, one pushing Break Hallway.

The plan

  1. Ash pushes Break Hallway to pull defenders off the wall.
  2. Open the Consul Office outside wall while they are looking at Ash.

Operators · first 3 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • ASAsh

Wall team, inside push and a flank watch.

  3. Plant in Consul Office with the hole covering you.

Operators · first 4 are your squad

Attack5-stack2FDraft · Y11S3

Full execute: wall, two pushes and a flank watch.

The plan

  1. 0:00 to 1:00: drone in and take Break Hallway.
  2. Thatcher EMPs and Thermite opens the Consul Office outside wall at about 1:30.
  3. Ash and Ying hit Break Hallway and Meeting Hallway together. Nomad holds Visa Stairs.

Operators · first 5 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • ASAsh
  • YIYing
  • NONomad

Common mistake

DefenceSolo2FDraft · Y11S3

Deny the Consul Office outside wall yourself and play from Meeting Room.

The plan

  1. Put your denial on the Consul Office outside wall so it survives even if your randoms do nothing.
  2. Reinforce the Consul Office outside wall before anything else.
  3. Call "Break Hallway" the moment you see a drone there.

Operators · first pick is you

  • KDKaid
  • Mute
  • Bandit
  • Mira

Common mistake

Roaming as a solo. Randoms rarely hold site while you are away.

DefenceDuo2FDraft · Y11S3

  2. Valkyrie cams cover Break Hallway and Meeting Hallway so you both see pushes early.
  3. Swap between Meeting Room and Consul Office when one side gets hit, and call it before you move.

AttackOperator setup2F

Where each attacker goes on Consul Office / Meeting Room

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Outside

    Breach the Consul Office outside wall from Outside. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Outside

    Backup breach on the Consul Office outside wall. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Outside

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Consul Office outside wall. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Outside

    EMP the Consul Office outside wall right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Break Hallway

    Lance the denial on the Consul Office outside wall from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Meeting Room.

  6. AS

    AshEntry · Break Hallway

    Take Break Hallway first and clear the doorway into Meeting Room for the plant.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Meeting Hallway

    Drive your shock drone into Meeting Room and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Visa Stairs

    Airjabs at Visa Stairs so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup2F

Where each defender goes on Consul Office / Meeting Room

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Consul Office

    Claw the Consul Office outside wall from inside Consul Office so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Break / Front Office wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Consul Office

    Sit on the Consul Office outside wall with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Meeting Room

    Jammer by the Break Hallway door into Meeting Room. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Meeting Room

    Mirror the wall between Meeting Room and Consul Office, so you can watch the plant in Consul Office from Meeting Room without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Meeting Room

    ADS on the Meeting Hallway side of Meeting Room. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Break Hallway

    Cams in Break Hallway and Meeting Hallway, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Meeting Room

    Anchor Meeting Room. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. VI

    VigilRoam · Visa Stairs

    Roam Visa Stairs and the rooms around it. Take one fight, then fall back to Meeting Room by 1:00.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Consul Office outside wall from Outside.
  • Take Break Hallway early and drone the site.
  • Clear Visa Stairs so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Consul Office outside wall stays denied, push Break Hallway and Meeting Hallway together with smokes and plant in Meeting Room.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Consul Office outside wall first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Break / Front Office wall next.
  • Watch Break Hallway and Meeting Hallway. That is where the inside push comes from.

If it goes wrong

If the Consul Office outside wall opens early, fall back into Meeting Room and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
