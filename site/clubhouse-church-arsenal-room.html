Where each attacker goes on Church / Arsenal Room

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Blue

    Breach the Arsenal Room wall to Blue from Blue. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Blue

    Backup breach on the Arsenal Room wall to Blue. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Blue

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Arsenal Room wall to Blue. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Blue

    EMP the Arsenal Room wall to Blue right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Church Hallway

    Lance the denial on the Arsenal Room wall to Blue from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Church.

  6. BU

    BuckVertical · 1F above

    Play Kitchen above Arsenal Room. Open small holes over the default anchor spots in Arsenal Room.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Bike Hallway

    Drive your shock drone into Church and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Main Stairs

    Airjabs at Main Stairs so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setupB

Where each defender goes on Church / Arsenal Room

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Arsenal Room

    Claw the Arsenal Room wall to Blue from inside Arsenal Room so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Church / Blue wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Arsenal Room

    Sit on the Arsenal Room wall to Blue with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Arsenal Room

    Jammer by the Church Hallway door into Arsenal Room. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Church

    Mirror the wall between Church and Arsenal Room, so you can watch the plant in Arsenal Room from Church without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Church

    ADS on the Bike Hallway side of Church. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Church Hallway

    Cams in Church Hallway and Bike Hallway, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Church

    Anchor Church. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. PU

    PulseVertical hunt · 1F above

    Go up to Kitchen above Arsenal Room early. Pulse attackers setting up vertical and pick them off before they open the floor.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Arsenal Room wall to Blue from Blue.
  • Play vertical from Kitchen above Arsenal Room to pin the anchors.
  • Clear Main Stairs so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Arsenal Room wall to Blue stays denied, push Church Hallway and Bike Hallway together with smokes and plant in Church.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Arsenal Room wall to Blue first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Church / Blue wall next.
  • Watch Kitchen above Arsenal Room. Attackers will play vertical from there.

If it goes wrong

If the Arsenal Room wall to Blue opens early, fall back into Church and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
