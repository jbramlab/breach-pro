AttackOperator setup1F

Where each attacker goes on Kitchen / Mechanic Shop

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Reception

    Breach the Kitchen wall to Reception from Reception. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Reception

    Backup breach on the Kitchen wall to Reception. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Reception

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Kitchen wall to Reception. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Reception

    EMP the Kitchen wall to Reception right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Kitchen Hallway

    Lance the denial on the Kitchen wall to Reception from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Mechanic Shop.

  6. BU

    BuckVertical · 2F above

    Play Office above Mechanic Shop. Open small holes over the default anchor spots in Mechanic Shop.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Garage Stairs

    Drive your shock drone into Mechanic Shop and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Reptile

    Airjabs at Reptile so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup1F

Where each defender goes on Kitchen / Mechanic Shop

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Kitchen

    Claw the Kitchen wall to Reception from inside Kitchen so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Reptile / Reception wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Kitchen

    Sit on the Kitchen wall to Reception with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Kitchen

    Jammer by the Kitchen Hallway door into Kitchen. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Mechanic Shop

    Mirror the wall between Mechanic Shop and Kitchen, so you can watch the plant in Kitchen from Mechanic Shop without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Mechanic Shop

    ADS on the Garage Stairs side of Mechanic Shop. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Kitchen Hallway

    Cams in Kitchen Hallway and Garage Stairs, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Mechanic Shop

    Anchor Mechanic Shop. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. PU

    PulseVertical hunt · 2F above

    Go up to Office above Mechanic Shop early. Pulse attackers setting up vertical and pick them off before they open the floor.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Kitchen wall to Reception from Reception.
  • Play vertical from Office above Mechanic Shop to pin the anchors.
  • Clear Reptile so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Kitchen wall to Reception stays denied, push Kitchen Hallway and Garage Stairs together with smokes and plant in Mechanic Shop.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Kitchen wall to Reception first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Reptile / Reception wall next.
  • Watch Office above Mechanic Shop. Attackers will play vertical from there.

If it goes wrong

If the Kitchen wall to Reception opens early, fall back into Mechanic Shop and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
