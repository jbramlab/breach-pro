AttackSolo2F · Gym / BedroomDraft · Y11S3

Bring your own breach or your own anti-denial.

The plan · numbers match the blueprint

  1. Ace or Maverick opens the Gym outside wall even with no help.
  2. Drone Construction for your randoms and call what you see.

  • Maverick

  • Kali

Deny the Gym outside wall yourself and play from Bedroom.

The plan · numbers match the blueprint

  1. Put your denial on the Gym outside wall so it survives even if your randoms do nothing.
  2. Reinforce the Gym outside wall before anything else.
  3. Call "Construction" the moment you see a drone there.

Attack basics, any squad size

  • Hard breach the Gym outside wall from 215.
  • Take Construction early and drone the site.
  • Clear Bathroom so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Gym outside wall stays denied, push Construction and Logistics together with smokes and plant in Bedroom.

What it looks like in game

Defence basics, any squad size

  • Reinforce the Gym outside wall first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Bedroom wall to Construction next.
  • Watch Construction and Logistics. That is where the inside push comes from.

If it goes wrong

If the Gym outside wall opens early, fall back into Bedroom and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
