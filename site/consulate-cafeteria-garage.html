You plus four randoms

  2. Drone Yellow Stairs for your randoms and call what you see.

  3. Call "Yellow Stairs" the moment you see a drone there.

AttackOperator setupB

Where each attacker goes on Cafeteria / Garage

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Pillar

    Breach the Cafeteria wall to Pillar from Pillar. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Pillar

    Backup breach on the Cafeteria wall to Pillar. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Pillar

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Cafeteria wall to Pillar. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Pillar

    EMP the Cafeteria wall to Pillar right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Yellow Stairs

    Lance the denial on the Cafeteria wall to Pillar from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Garage.

  6. BU

    BuckVertical · 1F above

    Play Piano Room above Garage. Open small holes over the default anchor spots in Garage.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Construction

    Drive your shock drone into Garage and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Server Stairs

    Airjabs at Server Stairs so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setupB

Where each defender goes on Cafeteria / Garage

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Cafeteria

    Claw the Cafeteria wall to Pillar from inside Cafeteria so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Storage / Servers wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Cafeteria

    Sit on the Cafeteria wall to Pillar with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Garage

    Jammer by the Yellow Stairs door into Garage. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Garage

    Mirror the wall between Garage and Cafeteria, so you can watch the plant in Cafeteria from Garage without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Cafeteria

    ADS on the Construction side of Cafeteria. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Yellow Stairs

    Cams in Yellow Stairs and Construction, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Garage

    Anchor Garage. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. PU

    PulseVertical hunt · 1F above

    Go up to Piano Room above Garage early. Pulse attackers setting up vertical and pick them off before they open the floor.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Cafeteria wall to Pillar from Pillar.
  • Play vertical from Piano Room above Garage to pin the anchors.
  • Clear Server Stairs so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Cafeteria wall to Pillar stays denied, push Yellow Stairs and Construction together with smokes and plant in Garage.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Cafeteria wall to Pillar first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Storage / Servers wall next.
  • Watch Piano Room above Garage. Attackers will play vertical from there.

If it goes wrong

If the Cafeteria wall to Pillar opens early, fall back into Garage and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
