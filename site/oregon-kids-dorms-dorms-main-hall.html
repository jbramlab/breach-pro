Kids' Dorms / Dorms Main Hall

You plus four randoms
AttackSolo2FDraft · Y11S3

Bring your own breach or your own anti-denial.

The plan

  1. Ace or Maverick opens the Dorms Main Hall wall to Low Attic even with no help.
  2. Drone White Stairs for your randoms and call what you see.

Operators · first pick is you

  • ACAce
  • Maverick
  • Thatcher
  • Kali

Common mistake

Breaching without droning, so the denier just stays alive.

AttackDuo2FDraft · Y11S3

Breacher plus support on the wall.

The plan

  1. Thatcher drones the wall, then EMPs the denier.
  2. Thermite opens the Dorms Main Hall wall to Low Attic and you both trade into Dorms Main Hall.

Operators · first 2 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • Hibana
  • Kali

Common mistake

Support forgetting to drone again after the EMP.

AttackTrio2FDraft · Y11S3

Two on the wall, one pushing White Stairs.

The plan

  1. Ash pushes White Stairs to pull defenders off the wall.
  2. Open the Dorms Main Hall wall to Low Attic while they are looking at Ash.

Operators · first 3 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • ASAsh

Common mistake

Entry going in before the wall is open and dying alone.

Attack4-stack2FDraft · Y11S3

Wall team, inside push and a flank watch.

The plan

  1. Nomad holds Gaming so nobody hits your backs.
  2. Ace and Kali open the Dorms Main Hall wall to Low Attic as Ash pushes White Stairs.
  3. Plant in Dorms Main Hall with the hole covering you.

Operators · first 4 are your squad

  • ACAce
  • KAKali
  • ASAsh
  • NONomad

Common mistake

Everyone on one side with nobody watching the flank.

Attack5-stack2FDraft · Y11S3

Full execute: wall, two pushes and a flank watch.

The plan

  1. 0:00 to 1:00: drone in and take White Stairs.
  2. Thatcher EMPs and Thermite opens the Dorms Main Hall wall to Low Attic at about 1:30.
  3. Ash and Ying hit White Stairs and Middle together. Nomad holds Gaming.

Operators · first 5 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • ASAsh
  • YIYing
  • NONomad

Common mistake

Opening the wall too early so defenders have time to reset.

DefenceSolo2FDraft · Y11S3

Deny the Dorms Main Hall wall to Low Attic yourself and play from Kids' Dorms.

The plan

  1. Put your denial on the Dorms Main Hall wall to Low Attic so it survives even if your randoms do nothing.
  2. Reinforce the Dorms Main Hall wall to Low Attic before anything else.
  3. Call "White Stairs" the moment you see a drone there.

Operators · first pick is you

  • KDKaid
  • Mute
  • Bandit
  • Mira

Common mistake

Roaming as a solo. Randoms rarely hold site while you are away.

DefenceDuo2FDraft · Y11S3

One anchor per room, one of you on cams.

The plan

  1. Kaid holds Dorms Main Hall and keeps the Dorms Main Hall wall to Low Attic electrified.
  2. Valkyrie cams cover White Stairs and Middle so you both see pushes early.
  3. Swap between Kids' Dorms and Dorms Main Hall when one side gets hit, and call it before you move.

Operators · first 2 are your squad

  • KDKaid
  • VAValkyrie
  • Bandit
  • Echo

Common mistake

Both of you watching the same doorway while the other room falls.

DefenceTrio2FDraft · Y11S3

Two anchors and a roamer in Gaming.

  2. Bandit keeps the Dorms Main Hall wall to Low Attic live. Smoke saves gas for the plant.

Deny the wall, delay in Gaming, then stop the plant.

  2. Valkyrie cams cover White Stairs and Middle.
  3. Vigil roams Gaming. Smoke holds gas until the defuser is down.

AttackOperator setup2F

Where each attacker goes on Kids' Dorms / Dorms Main Hall

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Low Attic

    Breach the Dorms Main Hall wall to Low Attic from Low Attic. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Low Attic

    Backup breach on the Dorms Main Hall wall to Low Attic. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Low Attic

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Dorms Main Hall wall to Low Attic. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Low Attic

    EMP the Dorms Main Hall wall to Low Attic right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · White Stairs

    Lance the denial on the Dorms Main Hall wall to Low Attic from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Kids' Dorms.

  6. AS

    AshEntry · White Stairs

    Take White Stairs first and clear the doorway into Kids' Dorms for the plant.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Middle

    Drive your shock drone into Kids' Dorms and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Gaming

    Airjabs at Gaming so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup2F

Where each defender goes on Kids' Dorms / Dorms Main Hall

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Dorms Main Hall

    Claw the Dorms Main Hall wall to Low Attic from inside Dorms Main Hall so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Armory / Armory Corridor wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Dorms Main Hall

    Sit on the Dorms Main Hall wall to Low Attic with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Kids' Dorms

    Jammer by the White Stairs door into Kids' Dorms. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Kids' Dorms

    Mirror the wall between Kids' Dorms and Dorms Main Hall, so you can watch the plant in Dorms Main Hall from Kids' Dorms without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Dorms Main Hall

    ADS on the Middle side of Dorms Main Hall. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · White Stairs

    Cams in White Stairs and Middle, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Kids' Dorms

    Anchor Kids' Dorms. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. VI

    VigilRoam · Gaming

    Roam Gaming and the rooms around it. Take one fight, then fall back to Kids' Dorms by 1:00.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Dorms Main Hall wall to Low Attic from Low Attic.
  • Take White Stairs early and drone the site.
  • Clear Gaming so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Dorms Main Hall wall to Low Attic stays denied, push White Stairs and Middle together with smokes and plant in Kids' Dorms.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Dorms Main Hall wall to Low Attic first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Armory / Armory Corridor wall next.
  • Watch White Stairs and Middle. That is where the inside push comes from.

If it goes wrong

If the Dorms Main Hall wall to Low Attic opens early, fall back into Kids' Dorms and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
