Mining Room / Fireplace Hall

You plus four randoms
AttackSolo2FDraft · Y11S3

Play vertical yourself. It works without team help.

The plan

  1. Get to White Corridor above Fireplace Hall and open holes over common anchor spots.
  2. Drone Mining Corridor for your randoms and call what you see.

Operators · first pick is you

  • BUBuck
  • Sledge
  • Ace
  • Zofia

Common mistake

Opening holes you never actually play from.

AttackDuo2FDraft · Y11S3

Breacher plus support on the wall.

The plan

  1. Thatcher drones the wall, then EMPs the denier.
  2. Thermite opens the Fireplace Hall wall to Reading Corridor and you both trade into Fireplace Hall.

Operators · first 2 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • Hibana
  • Kali

Common mistake

Support forgetting to drone again after the EMP.

AttackTrio2FDraft · Y11S3

Wall, vertical and support.

The plan

  1. Buck pins anchors from White Corridor above Fireplace Hall.
  2. Thatcher EMPs and Thermite opens the Fireplace Hall wall to Reading Corridor while anchors look up.

Operators · first 3 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • BUBuck

Common mistake

Breaching before the vertical player is set.

Attack4-stack2FDraft · Y11S3

Wall team, inside push and a flank watch.

The plan

  1. Nomad holds Red Stairs so nobody hits your backs.
  2. Ace and Kali open the Fireplace Hall wall to Reading Corridor as Ash pushes Mining Corridor.
  3. Plant in Fireplace Hall with the hole covering you.

Operators · first 4 are your squad

  • ACAce
  • KAKali
  • ASAsh
  • NONomad

Common mistake

Everyone on one side with nobody watching the flank.

Attack5-stack2FDraft · Y11S3

Full execute: wall, vertical, inside push and a flank watch.

The plan

  1. 0:00 to 1:00: drone in and take Mining Corridor.
  2. Thatcher EMPs and Thermite opens the Fireplace Hall wall to Reading Corridor at about 1:30.
  3. Buck plays vertical as Ash pushes. Nomad holds Red Stairs.

Operators · first 5 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • BUBuck
  • ASAsh
  • NONomad

Common mistake

Opening the wall too early so defenders have time to reset.

DefenceSolo2FDraft · Y11S3

Deny the Fireplace Hall wall to Reading Corridor yourself and play from Mining Room.

The plan

  1. Put your denial on the Fireplace Hall wall to Reading Corridor so it survives even if your randoms do nothing.
  2. Reinforce the Fireplace Hall wall to Reading Corridor before anything else.
  3. Call "Mining Corridor" the moment you see a drone there.

Operators · first pick is you

  • KDKaid
  • Mute
  • Bandit
  • Mira

Common mistake

Roaming as a solo. Randoms rarely hold site while you are away.

DefenceDuo2FDraft · Y11S3

One anchor per room, one of you on cams.

The plan

  1. Kaid holds Fireplace Hall and keeps the Fireplace Hall wall to Reading Corridor electrified.
  2. Valkyrie cams cover Mining Corridor and Red Corridor so you both see pushes early.
  3. Swap between Mining Room and Fireplace Hall when one side gets hit, and call it before you move.

Operators · first 2 are your squad

  • KDKaid
  • VAValkyrie
  • Bandit
  • Echo

Common mistake

Both of you watching the same doorway while the other room falls.

AttackOperator setup2F

Where each attacker goes on Mining Room / Fireplace Hall

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Reading Corridor

    Breach the Fireplace Hall wall to Reading Corridor from Reading Corridor. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Reading Corridor

    Backup breach on the Fireplace Hall wall to Reading Corridor. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Reading Corridor

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Fireplace Hall wall to Reading Corridor. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Reading Corridor

    EMP the Fireplace Hall wall to Reading Corridor right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Mining Corridor

    Lance the denial on the Fireplace Hall wall to Reading Corridor from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Mining Room.

  6. BU

    BuckVertical · 3F above

    Play White Corridor above Fireplace Hall. Open small holes over the default anchor spots in Fireplace Hall.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Red Corridor

    Drive your shock drone into Mining Room and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Red Stairs

    Airjabs at Red Stairs so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup2F

Where each defender goes on Mining Room / Fireplace Hall

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Fireplace Hall

    Claw the Fireplace Hall wall to Reading Corridor from inside Fireplace Hall so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Reading Corridor / White Stairs wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Fireplace Hall

    Sit on the Fireplace Hall wall to Reading Corridor with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Fireplace Hall

    Jammer by the Mining Corridor door into Fireplace Hall. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Mining Room

    Mirror the wall between Mining Room and Fireplace Hall, so you can watch the plant in Fireplace Hall from Mining Room without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Mining Room

    ADS on the Red Corridor side of Mining Room. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Mining Corridor

    Cams in Mining Corridor and Red Corridor, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Mining Room

    Anchor Mining Room. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. PU

    PulseVertical hunt · 3F above

    Go up to White Corridor above Fireplace Hall early. Pulse attackers setting up vertical and pick them off before they open the floor.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Fireplace Hall wall to Reading Corridor from Reading Corridor.
  • Play vertical from White Corridor above Fireplace Hall to pin the anchors.
  • Clear Red Stairs so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Fireplace Hall wall to Reading Corridor stays denied, push Mining Corridor and Red Corridor together with smokes and plant in Mining Room.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Fireplace Hall wall to Reading Corridor first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Reading Corridor / White Stairs wall next.
  • Watch White Corridor above Fireplace Hall. Attackers will play vertical from there.

If it goes wrong

If the Fireplace Hall wall to Reading Corridor opens early, fall back into Mining Room and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
