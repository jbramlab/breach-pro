AttackOperator setup1F

Where each attacker goes on Dining Room / Kitchen

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Dining Hallway

    Breach the Dining Room wall to Dining Hallway from Dining Hallway. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Dining Hallway

    Backup breach on the Dining Room wall to Dining Hallway. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Dining Hallway

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Dining Room wall to Dining Hallway. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Dining Hallway

    EMP the Dining Room wall to Dining Hallway right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Main

    Lance the denial on the Dining Room wall to Dining Hallway from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Kitchen.

  6. BU

    BuckVertical · 2F above

    Play Master Bedroom above Kitchen. Open small holes over the default anchor spots in Kitchen.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Kitchen Hallway

    Drive your shock drone into Kitchen and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Trophy

    Airjabs at Trophy so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup1F

Where each defender goes on Dining Room / Kitchen

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Dining Room

    Claw the Dining Room wall to Dining Hallway from inside Dining Room so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Dining Room / Fireplace wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Dining Room

    Sit on the Dining Room wall to Dining Hallway with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Dining Room

    Jammer by the Main door into Dining Room. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Kitchen

    Mirror the wall between Kitchen and Dining Room, so you can watch the plant in Dining Room from Kitchen without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Dining Room

    ADS on the Kitchen Hallway side of Dining Room. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Main

    Cams in Main and Kitchen Hallway, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Kitchen

    Anchor Kitchen. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. PU

    PulseVertical hunt · 2F above

    Go up to Master Bedroom above Kitchen early. Pulse attackers setting up vertical and pick them off before they open the floor.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Dining Room wall to Dining Hallway from Dining Hallway.
  • Play vertical from Master Bedroom above Kitchen to pin the anchors.
  • Clear Trophy so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Dining Room wall to Dining Hallway stays denied, push Main and Kitchen Hallway together with smokes and plant in Kitchen.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Dining Room wall to Dining Hallway first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Dining Room / Fireplace wall next.
  • Watch Master Bedroom above Kitchen. Attackers will play vertical from there.

If it goes wrong

If the Dining Room wall to Dining Hallway opens early, fall back into Kitchen and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
