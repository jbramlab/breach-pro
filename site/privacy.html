Privacy and cookies

This page explains what data Breach Pro collects and why. Last updated 15 October 2026.

What we store on your device

The site remembers your last chosen side and squad size in your browser's local storage, so the next page opens the way you left it. This never leaves your device and you can clear it in your browser settings.

Advertising

We may show ads served by Google AdSense to keep the site free. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. In the UK and EEA you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from the privacy link in the consent message. You can manage Google ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com. Read how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Fonts

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask what data we hold about you and ask us to delete it. We do not hold accounts or personal details. Contact contact@breachpro.co.uk.
