Attack basics, any squad size

  • Hard breach the Church wall to Blue from Blue.
  • Open the Kitchen hatch for pressure from above.
  • Clear Blue stairs so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Church wall to Blue stays denied, push Hallway and Dirt tunnel together with smokes and plant in Arsenal.

What it looks like in game

Defence basics, any squad size

  • Reinforce the Church wall to Blue first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Kitchen hatch so attackers cannot drop in.
  • Watch Hallway and Dirt tunnel. That is where the inside push comes from.

If it goes wrong

If the Church wall to Blue opens early, fall back into Arsenal and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
