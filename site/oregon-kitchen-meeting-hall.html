Kitchen / Meeting Hall

You plus four randoms
AttackSolo1FDraft · Y11S3

Play vertical yourself. It works without team help.

The plan

  1. Get to Dorms Main Hall above Kitchen and open holes over common anchor spots.
  2. Drone Kitchen Corridor for your randoms and call what you see.

  • Sledge
  • Ace
  • Zofia

AttackOperator setup1F

Where each attacker goes on Kitchen / Meeting Hall

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Lobby

    Breach the Meeting Hall wall to Lobby from Lobby. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Lobby

    Backup breach on the Meeting Hall wall to Lobby. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Lobby

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Meeting Hall wall to Lobby. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Lobby

    EMP the Meeting Hall wall to Lobby right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Kitchen Corridor

    Lance the denial on the Meeting Hall wall to Lobby from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Kitchen.

  6. BU

    BuckVertical · 2F above

    Play Dorms Main Hall above Kitchen. Open small holes over the default anchor spots in Kitchen.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Security Corridor

    Drive your shock drone into Kitchen and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · White Stairs

    Airjabs at White Stairs so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup1F

Where each defender goes on Kitchen / Meeting Hall

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Meeting Hall

    Claw the Meeting Hall wall to Lobby from inside Meeting Hall so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Dining Hall / Kitchen wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Meeting Hall

    Sit on the Meeting Hall wall to Lobby with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Kitchen

    Jammer by the Kitchen Corridor door into Kitchen. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Kitchen

    Mirror the wall between Kitchen and Meeting Hall, so you can watch the plant in Meeting Hall from Kitchen without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Kitchen

    ADS on the Security Corridor side of Kitchen. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Kitchen Corridor

    Cams in Kitchen Corridor and Security Corridor, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Kitchen

    Anchor Kitchen. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. PU

    PulseVertical hunt · 2F above

    Go up to Dorms Main Hall above Kitchen early. Pulse attackers setting up vertical and pick them off before they open the floor.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Meeting Hall wall to Lobby from Lobby.
  • Play vertical from Dorms Main Hall above Kitchen to pin the anchors.
  • Clear White Stairs so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Meeting Hall wall to Lobby stays denied, push Kitchen Corridor and Security Corridor together with smokes and plant in Kitchen.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Meeting Hall wall to Lobby first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Dining Hall / Kitchen wall next.
  • Watch Dorms Main Hall above Kitchen. Attackers will play vertical from there.

If it goes wrong

If the Meeting Hall wall to Lobby opens early, fall back into Kitchen and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
