Penthouse / Theater

Best guess The two bomb rooms on this plan are our best guess from the blueprint. Check it in a custom game before you rely on it.

You plus four randoms
AttackSolo2FDraft · Y11S3

Bring your own breach or your own anti-denial.

The plan

  1. Ace or Maverick opens the Penthouse outside wall even with no help.
  2. Drone TV Hallway for your randoms and call what you see.

Operators · first pick is you

  • ACAce
  • Maverick
  • Thatcher
  • Kali

Common mistake

Breaching without droning, so the denier just stays alive.

AttackDuo2FDraft · Y11S3

Breacher plus support on the wall.

The plan

  1. Thatcher drones the wall, then EMPs the denier.
  2. Thermite opens the Penthouse outside wall and you both trade into Penthouse.

Operators · first 2 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • Hibana
  • Kali

Common mistake

Support forgetting to drone again after the EMP.

AttackTrio2FDraft · Y11S3

Two on the wall, one pushing TV Hallway.

The plan

  1. Ash pushes TV Hallway to pull defenders off the wall.
  2. Open the Penthouse outside wall while they are looking at Ash.

Operators · first 3 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • ASAsh

Wall team, inside push and a flank watch.

  2. Ace and Kali open the Penthouse outside wall as Ash pushes TV Hallway.
  3. Plant in Penthouse with the hole covering you.

  2. Thatcher EMPs and Thermite opens the Penthouse outside wall at about 1:30.
  3. Ash and Ying hit TV Hallway and Blue Stairs together. Nomad holds VIP Hallway.

DefenceSolo2FDraft · Y11S3

Deny the Penthouse outside wall yourself and play from Theater.

The plan

  1. Put your denial on the Penthouse outside wall so it survives even if your randoms do nothing.
  2. Reinforce the Penthouse outside wall before anything else.
  3. Call "TV Hallway" the moment you see a drone there.

Operators · first pick is you

  • Bandit
  • Mira

AttackOperator setup2F

Where each attacker goes on Penthouse / Theater

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Outside

    Breach the Penthouse outside wall from Outside. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Outside

    Backup breach on the Penthouse outside wall. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Outside

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Penthouse outside wall. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Outside

    EMP the Penthouse outside wall right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · TV Hallway

    Lance the denial on the Penthouse outside wall from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Theater.

  6. AS

    AshEntry · TV Hallway

    Take TV Hallway first and clear the doorway into Penthouse for the plant.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Blue Stairs

    Drive your shock drone into Theater and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · VIP Hallway

    Airjabs at VIP Hallway so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup2F

Where each defender goes on Penthouse / Theater

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Penthouse

    Claw the Penthouse outside wall from inside Penthouse so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the TV Hallway outside wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Penthouse

    Sit on the Penthouse outside wall with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Penthouse

    Jammer by the TV Hallway door into Penthouse. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Theater

    Mirror the wall between Theater and Penthouse, so you can watch the plant in Penthouse from Theater without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Theater

    ADS on the Blue Stairs side of Theater. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · TV Hallway

    Cams in TV Hallway and Blue Stairs, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Theater

    Anchor Theater. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. VI

    VigilRoam · VIP Hallway

    Roam VIP Hallway and the rooms around it. Take one fight, then fall back to Theater by 1:00.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Penthouse outside wall from Outside.
  • Take TV Hallway early and drone the site.
  • Clear VIP Hallway so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Penthouse outside wall stays denied, push TV Hallway and Blue Stairs together with smokes and plant in Theater.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Penthouse outside wall first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the TV Hallway outside wall next.
  • Watch TV Hallway and Blue Stairs. That is where the inside push comes from.

If it goes wrong

If the Penthouse outside wall opens early, fall back into Theater and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
