Executive Lounge / CEO Office

Best guess The two bomb rooms on this plan are our best guess from the blueprint. Check it in a custom game before you rely on it.

You plus four randoms
AttackSolo2FDraft · Y11S3

Bring your own breach or your own anti-denial.

The plan

  1. Ace or Maverick opens the CEO Office wall to Square Stairs even with no help.
  2. Drone Exec Hallway for your randoms and call what you see.

Operators · first pick is you

  • ACAce
  • Maverick
  • Thatcher
  • Kali

Common mistake

Breaching without droning, so the denier just stays alive.

  3. Call "Exec Hallway" the moment you see a drone there.

AttackOperator setup2F

Where each attacker goes on Executive Lounge / CEO Office

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Square Stairs

    Breach the CEO Office wall to Square Stairs from Square Stairs. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Square Stairs

    Backup breach on the CEO Office wall to Square Stairs. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Square Stairs

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the CEO Office wall to Square Stairs. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Square Stairs

    EMP the CEO Office wall to Square Stairs right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Exec Hallway

    Lance the denial on the CEO Office wall to Square Stairs from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Executive Lounge.

  6. AS

    AshEntry · Exec Hallway

    Take Exec Hallway first and clear the doorway into Executive Lounge for the plant.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Front Desk

    Drive your shock drone into Executive Lounge and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Lobby Stairs

    Airjabs at Lobby Stairs so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup2F

Where each defender goes on Executive Lounge / CEO Office

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · CEO Office

    Claw the CEO Office wall to Square Stairs from inside CEO Office so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Loan / Elevators wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · CEO Office

    Sit on the CEO Office wall to Square Stairs with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Executive Lounge

    Jammer by the Exec Hallway door into Executive Lounge. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Executive Lounge

    Mirror the wall between Executive Lounge and CEO Office, so you can watch the plant in CEO Office from Executive Lounge without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Executive Lounge

    ADS on the Front Desk side of Executive Lounge. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Exec Hallway

    Cams in Exec Hallway and Front Desk, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Executive Lounge

    Anchor Executive Lounge. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. VI

    VigilRoam · Lobby Stairs

    Roam Lobby Stairs and the rooms around it. Take one fight, then fall back to Executive Lounge by 1:00.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the CEO Office wall to Square Stairs from Square Stairs.
  • Take Exec Hallway early and drone the site.
  • Clear Lobby Stairs so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the CEO Office wall to Square Stairs stays denied, push Exec Hallway and Front Desk together with smokes and plant in CEO Office.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the CEO Office wall to Square Stairs first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Loan / Elevators wall next.
  • Watch Exec Hallway and Front Desk. That is where the inside push comes from.

If it goes wrong

If the CEO Office wall to Square Stairs opens early, fall back into CEO Office and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
