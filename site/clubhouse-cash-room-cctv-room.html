Cash Room / CCTV Room

You plus four randoms
AttackSolo2FDraft · Y11S3

Bring your own breach or your own anti-denial.

The plan

  1. Ace or Maverick opens the CCTV Room wall to Catwalk even with no help.
  2. Drone Red Stairs for your randoms and call what you see.

Operators · first pick is you

  • ACAce
  • Maverick
  • Thatcher
  • Kali

Common mistake

Breaching without droning, so the denier just stays alive.

AttackDuo2FDraft · Y11S3

Breacher plus support on the wall.

The plan

  1. Thatcher drones the wall, then EMPs the denier.
  2. Thermite opens the CCTV Room wall to Catwalk and you both trade into CCTV Room.

Operators · first 2 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • Hibana
  • Kali

Common mistake

Support forgetting to drone again after the EMP.

AttackTrio2FDraft · Y11S3

Two on the wall, one pushing Red Stairs.

The plan

  1. Ash pushes Red Stairs to pull defenders off the wall.
  2. Open the CCTV Room wall to Catwalk while they are looking at Ash.

Operators · first 3 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • ASAsh

Common mistake

Entry going in before the wall is open and dying alone.

Attack4-stack2FDraft · Y11S3

Wall team, inside push and a flank watch.

The plan

  1. Nomad holds Secret so nobody hits your backs.
  2. Ace and Kali open the CCTV Room wall to Catwalk as Ash pushes Red Stairs.
  3. Plant in CCTV Room with the hole covering you.

Operators · first 4 are your squad

  • ACAce
  • KAKali
  • ASAsh
  • NONomad

Everyone on one side with nobody watching the flank.

Attack5-stack2FDraft · Y11S3

Full execute: wall, two pushes and a flank watch.

The plan

  1. 0:00 to 1:00: drone in and take Red Stairs.
  2. Thatcher EMPs and Thermite opens the CCTV Room wall to Catwalk at about 1:30.
  3. Ash and Ying hit Red Stairs and Construction together. Nomad holds Secret.

Operators · first 5 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • ASAsh
  • YIYing
  • NONomad

Common mistake

Opening the wall too early so defenders have time to reset.

DefenceSolo2FDraft · Y11S3

Deny the CCTV Room wall to Catwalk yourself and play from Cash Room.

The plan

  1. Put your denial on the CCTV Room wall to Catwalk so it survives even if your randoms do nothing.
  2. Reinforce the CCTV Room wall to Catwalk before anything else.
  3. Call "Red Stairs" the moment you see a drone there.

Operators · first pick is you

  • KDKaid
  • Mute
  • Bandit
  • Mira

Common mistake

Roaming as a solo. Randoms rarely hold site while you are away.

DefenceDuo2FDraft · Y11S3

One anchor per room, one of you on cams.

The plan

  1. Kaid holds CCTV Room and keeps the CCTV Room wall to Catwalk electrified.
  2. Valkyrie cams cover Red Stairs and Construction so you both see pushes early.
  3. Swap between Cash Room and CCTV Room when one side gets hit, and call it before you move.

  • KDKaid
  • VAValkyrie
  • Bandit
  • Echo

Common mistake

AttackOperator setup2F

Where each attacker goes on Cash Room / CCTV Room

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Catwalk

    Breach the CCTV Room wall to Catwalk from Catwalk. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Catwalk

    Backup breach on the CCTV Room wall to Catwalk. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Catwalk

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the CCTV Room wall to Catwalk. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Catwalk

    EMP the CCTV Room wall to Catwalk right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Red Stairs

    Lance the denial on the CCTV Room wall to Catwalk from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Cash Room.

  6. AS

    AshEntry · Red Stairs

    Take Red Stairs first and clear the doorway into Cash Room for the plant.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Construction

    Drive your shock drone into Cash Room and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Secret

    Airjabs at Secret so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup2F

Where each defender goes on Cash Room / CCTV Room

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · CCTV Room

    Claw the CCTV Room wall to Catwalk from inside CCTV Room so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Construction / Secret wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · CCTV Room

    Sit on the CCTV Room wall to Catwalk with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Cash Room

    Jammer by the Red Stairs door into Cash Room. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Cash Room

    Mirror the wall between Cash Room and CCTV Room, so you can watch the plant in CCTV Room from Cash Room without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Cash Room

    ADS on the Construction side of Cash Room. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Red Stairs

    Cams in Red Stairs and Construction, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Cash Room

    Anchor Cash Room. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. VI

    VigilRoam · Secret

    Roam Secret and the rooms around it. Take one fight, then fall back to Cash Room by 1:00.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the CCTV Room wall to Catwalk from Catwalk.
  • Take Red Stairs early and drone the site.
  • Clear Secret so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the CCTV Room wall to Catwalk stays denied, push Red Stairs and Construction together with smokes and plant in Cash Room.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the CCTV Room wall to Catwalk first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Construction / Secret wall next.
  • Watch Red Stairs and Construction. That is where the inside push comes from.

If it goes wrong

If the CCTV Room wall to Catwalk opens early, fall back into Cash Room and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
