Waiting Room / Cafeteria

Best guess The two bomb rooms on this plan are our best guess from the blueprint. Check it in a custom game before you rely on it.

  2. Drone Fountain Hallway for your randoms and call what you see.

AttackOperator setup1F

Where each attacker goes on Waiting Room / Cafeteria

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Infirmary

    Breach the Cafeteria wall to Infirmary from Infirmary. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Infirmary

    Backup breach on the Cafeteria wall to Infirmary. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Infirmary

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Cafeteria wall to Infirmary. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Infirmary

    EMP the Cafeteria wall to Infirmary right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Fountain Hallway

    Lance the denial on the Cafeteria wall to Infirmary from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Waiting Room.

  6. BU

    BuckVertical · 2F above

    Play Dormitory above Cafeteria. Open small holes over the default anchor spots in Cafeteria.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Main Hallway

    Drive your shock drone into Waiting Room and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Main Stairs

    Airjabs at Main Stairs so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup1F

Where each defender goes on Waiting Room / Cafeteria

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Cafeteria

    Claw the Cafeteria wall to Infirmary from inside Cafeteria so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Kitchen Hallway / Kitchen wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Cafeteria

    Sit on the Cafeteria wall to Infirmary with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Waiting Room

    Jammer by the Fountain Hallway door into Waiting Room. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Waiting Room

    Mirror the wall between Waiting Room and Cafeteria, so you can watch the plant in Cafeteria from Waiting Room without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Cafeteria

    ADS on the Main Hallway side of Cafeteria. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Fountain Hallway

    Cams in Fountain Hallway and Main Hallway, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Waiting Room

    Anchor Waiting Room. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. PU

    PulseVertical hunt · 2F above

    Go up to Dormitory above Cafeteria early. Pulse attackers setting up vertical and pick them off before they open the floor.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Cafeteria wall to Infirmary from Infirmary.
  • Play vertical from Dormitory above Cafeteria to pin the anchors.
  • Clear Main Stairs so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Cafeteria wall to Infirmary stays denied, push Fountain Hallway and Main Hallway together with smokes and plant in Waiting Room.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Cafeteria wall to Infirmary first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Kitchen Hallway / Kitchen wall next.
  • Watch Dormitory above Cafeteria. Attackers will play vertical from there.

If it goes wrong

If the Cafeteria wall to Infirmary opens early, fall back into Waiting Room and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
