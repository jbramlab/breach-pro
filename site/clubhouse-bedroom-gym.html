Clubhouse · 2F · Site 1 of 4

Bedroom / Gym

You plus four randoms
AttackSolo2FDraft · Y11S3

Bring your own breach or your own anti-denial.

The plan

  1. Ace or Maverick opens the Bedroom outside wall even with no help.
  2. Drone Bedroom Hallway for your randoms and call what you see.

Operators · first pick is you

  • ACAce
  • Maverick
  • Thatcher
  • Kali

Common mistake

Breaching without droning, so the denier just stays alive.

AttackDuo2FDraft · Y11S3

Breacher plus support on the wall.

The plan

  1. Thatcher drones the wall, then EMPs the denier.
  2. Thermite opens the Bedroom outside wall and you both trade into Bedroom.

Operators · first 2 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • Hibana
  • Kali

Common mistake

Support forgetting to drone again after the EMP.

AttackTrio2FDraft · Y11S3

Two on the wall, one pushing Bedroom Hallway.

The plan

  1. Ash pushes Bedroom Hallway to pull defenders off the wall.
  2. Open the Bedroom outside wall while they are looking at Ash.

Operators · first 3 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • ASAsh

Common mistake

Attack4-stack2FDraft · Y11S3

Wall team, inside push and a flank watch.

The plan

  1. Nomad holds Main Stairs so nobody hits your backs.
  2. Ace and Kali open the Bedroom outside wall as Ash pushes Bedroom Hallway.
  3. Plant in Bedroom with the hole covering you.

Operators · first 4 are your squad

  • ACAce
  • KAKali
  • ASAsh
  • NONomad

Common mistake

Everyone on one side with nobody watching the flank.

Attack5-stack2FDraft · Y11S3

Full execute: wall, two pushes and a flank watch.

The plan

  1. 0:00 to 1:00: drone in and take Bedroom Hallway.
  2. Thatcher EMPs and Thermite opens the Bedroom outside wall at about 1:30.
  3. Ash and Ying hit Bedroom Hallway and Gym Hallway together. Nomad holds Main Stairs.

Operators · first 5 are your squad

  • TMThermite
  • THThatcher
  • ASAsh
  • YIYing
  • NONomad

Common mistake

Opening the wall too early so defenders have time to reset.

DefenceSolo2FDraft · Y11S3

Deny the Bedroom outside wall yourself and play from Gym.

The plan

  1. Put your denial on the Bedroom outside wall so it survives even if your randoms do nothing.
  2. Reinforce the Bedroom outside wall before anything else.
  3. Call "Bedroom Hallway" the moment you see a drone there.

Operators · first pick is you

  • KDKaid
  • Mute
  • Bandit
  • Mira

Common mistake

Roaming as a solo. Randoms rarely hold site while you are away.

DefenceDuo2FDraft · Y11S3

One anchor per room, one of you on cams.

The plan

  1. Kaid holds Bedroom and keeps the Bedroom outside wall electrified.
  2. Valkyrie cams cover Bedroom Hallway and Gym Hallway so you both see pushes early.
  3. Swap between Bedroom and Gym when one side gets hit, and call it before you move.

  • KDKaid
  • VAValkyrie
  • Bandit
  • Echo

Common mistake

AttackOperator setup2F

Where each attacker goes on Bedroom / Gym

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Outside

    Breach the Bedroom outside wall from Outside. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Outside

    Backup breach on the Bedroom outside wall. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Outside

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Bedroom outside wall. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Outside

    EMP the Bedroom outside wall right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Bedroom Hallway

    Lance the denial on the Bedroom outside wall from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Gym.

  6. AS

    AshEntry · Bedroom Hallway

    Take Bedroom Hallway first and clear the doorway into Bedroom for the plant.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Gym Hallway

    Drive your shock drone into Gym and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Main Stairs

    Airjabs at Main Stairs so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup2F

Where each defender goes on Bedroom / Gym

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Bedroom

    Claw the Bedroom outside wall from inside Bedroom so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Cash / CCTV wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Bedroom

    Sit on the Bedroom outside wall with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Bedroom

    Jammer by the Bedroom Hallway door into Bedroom. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Gym

    Mirror the wall between Gym and Bedroom, so you can watch the plant in Bedroom from Gym without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Gym

    ADS on the Gym Hallway side of Gym. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Bedroom Hallway

    Cams in Bedroom Hallway and Gym Hallway, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Gym

    Anchor Gym. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. VI

    VigilRoam · Main Stairs

    Roam Main Stairs and the rooms around it. Take one fight, then fall back to Gym by 1:00.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Bedroom outside wall from Outside.
  • Take Bedroom Hallway early and drone the site.
  • Clear Main Stairs so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Bedroom outside wall stays denied, push Bedroom Hallway and Gym Hallway together with smokes and plant in Gym.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Bedroom outside wall first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Cash / CCTV wall next.
  • Watch Bedroom Hallway and Gym Hallway. That is where the inside push comes from.

If it goes wrong

If the Bedroom outside wall opens early, fall back into Gym and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
