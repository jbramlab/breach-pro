Dining Hall / Kitchen

You plus four randoms
AttackSolo1FDraft · Y11S3

Play vertical yourself. It works without team help.

The plan

  1. Get to Dorms Main Hall above Kitchen and open holes over common anchor spots.
  2. Drone Shower Corridor for your randoms and call what you see.

Operators · first pick is you

  • BUBuck
  • Sledge
  • Ace
  • Zofia

Common mistake

Opening holes you never actually play from.

  2. Thermite opens the Dining Hall wall to Showers and you both trade into Dining Hall.

  • THThatcher
  • Hibana
  • Kali

  2. Thatcher EMPs and Thermite opens the Dining Hall wall to Showers at about 1:30.

DefenceSolo1FDraft · Y11S3

Deny the Dining Hall wall to Showers yourself and play from Kitchen.

The plan

  1. Put your denial on the Dining Hall wall to Showers so it survives even if your randoms do nothing.
  2. Reinforce the Dining Hall wall to Showers before anything else.
  3. Call "Shower Corridor" the moment you see a drone there.

Operators · first pick is you

  • KDKaid
  • Mute
  • Bandit
  • Mira

Common mistake

Roaming as a solo. Randoms rarely hold site while you are away.

  2. Valkyrie cams cover Shower Corridor and Small Stairs so you both see pushes early.
  3. Swap between Dining Hall and Kitchen when one side gets hit, and call it before you move.

AttackOperator setup1F

Where each attacker goes on Dining Hall / Kitchen

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Showers

    Breach the Dining Hall wall to Showers from Showers. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Showers

    Backup breach on the Dining Hall wall to Showers. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Showers

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Dining Hall wall to Showers. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Showers

    EMP the Dining Hall wall to Showers right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Shower Corridor

    Lance the denial on the Dining Hall wall to Showers from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Kitchen.

  6. BU

    BuckVertical · 2F above

    Play Dorms Main Hall above Kitchen. Open small holes over the default anchor spots in Kitchen.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Small Stairs

    Drive your shock drone into Kitchen and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · White Stairs

    Airjabs at White Stairs so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup1F

Where each defender goes on Dining Hall / Kitchen

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Dining Hall

    Claw the Dining Hall wall to Showers from inside Dining Hall so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Kitchen / Meeting wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Dining Hall

    Sit on the Dining Hall wall to Showers with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Dining Hall

    Jammer by the Shower Corridor door into Dining Hall. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Kitchen

    Mirror the wall between Kitchen and Dining Hall, so you can watch the plant in Dining Hall from Kitchen without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Dining Hall

    ADS on the Small Stairs side of Dining Hall. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Shower Corridor

    Cams in Shower Corridor and Small Stairs, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Kitchen

    Anchor Kitchen. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. PU

    PulseVertical hunt · 2F above

    Go up to Dorms Main Hall above Kitchen early. Pulse attackers setting up vertical and pick them off before they open the floor.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Dining Hall wall to Showers from Showers.
  • Play vertical from Dorms Main Hall above Kitchen to pin the anchors.
  • Clear White Stairs so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Dining Hall wall to Showers stays denied, push Shower Corridor and Small Stairs together with smokes and plant in Kitchen.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Dining Hall wall to Showers first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Kitchen / Meeting wall next.
  • Watch Dorms Main Hall above Kitchen. Attackers will play vertical from there.

If it goes wrong

If the Dining Hall wall to Showers opens early, fall back into Kitchen and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
