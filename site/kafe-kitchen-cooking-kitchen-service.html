Kitchen Cooking / Kitchen Service

You plus four randoms
AttackSolo1FDraft · Y11S3

Play vertical yourself. It works without team help.

The plan

  1. Get to Reading Corridor above Kitchen Service and open holes over common anchor spots.
  2. Drone VIP Corridor for your randoms and call what you see.

Operators · first pick is you

  • BUBuck
  • Sledge
  • Ace
  • Zofia

Opening holes you never actually play from.

  2. Thermite opens the Kitchen Cooking wall to Bakery Corridor and you both trade into Kitchen Cooking.

DefenceSolo1FDraft · Y11S3

Deny the Kitchen Cooking wall to Bakery Corridor yourself and play from Kitchen Service.

The plan

  1. Put your denial on the Kitchen Cooking wall to Bakery Corridor so it survives even if your randoms do nothing.
  2. Reinforce the Kitchen Cooking wall to Bakery Corridor before anything else.
  3. Call "VIP Corridor" the moment you see a drone there.

  • Mute

  • Mira

AttackOperator setup1F

Where each attacker goes on Kitchen Cooking / Kitchen Service

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Bakery Corridor

    Breach the Kitchen Cooking wall to Bakery Corridor from Bakery Corridor. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Bakery Corridor

    Backup breach on the Kitchen Cooking wall to Bakery Corridor. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Bakery Corridor

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Kitchen Cooking wall to Bakery Corridor. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Bakery Corridor

    EMP the Kitchen Cooking wall to Bakery Corridor right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · VIP Corridor

    Lance the denial on the Kitchen Cooking wall to Bakery Corridor from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Kitchen Service.

  6. BU

    BuckVertical · 2F above

    Play Reading Corridor above Kitchen Service. Open small holes over the default anchor spots in Kitchen Service.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Bakery

    Drive your shock drone into Kitchen Service and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Bottom Bar

    Airjabs at Bottom Bar so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup1F

Where each defender goes on Kitchen Cooking / Kitchen Service

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Kitchen Cooking

    Claw the Kitchen Cooking wall to Bakery Corridor from inside Kitchen Cooking so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Prep outside wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Kitchen Cooking

    Sit on the Kitchen Cooking wall to Bakery Corridor with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Kitchen Service

    Jammer by the VIP Corridor door into Kitchen Service. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Kitchen Service

    Mirror the wall between Kitchen Service and Kitchen Cooking, so you can watch the plant in Kitchen Cooking from Kitchen Service without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Kitchen Cooking

    ADS on the Bakery side of Kitchen Cooking. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · VIP Corridor

    Cams in VIP Corridor and Bakery, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Kitchen Service

    Anchor Kitchen Service. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. PU

    PulseVertical hunt · 2F above

    Go up to Reading Corridor above Kitchen Service early. Pulse attackers setting up vertical and pick them off before they open the floor.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Kitchen Cooking wall to Bakery Corridor from Bakery Corridor.
  • Play vertical from Reading Corridor above Kitchen Service to pin the anchors.
  • Clear Bottom Bar so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Kitchen Cooking wall to Bakery Corridor stays denied, push VIP Corridor and Bakery together with smokes and plant in Kitchen Service.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Kitchen Cooking wall to Bakery Corridor first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Prep outside wall next.
  • Watch Reading Corridor above Kitchen Service. Attackers will play vertical from there.

If it goes wrong

If the Kitchen Cooking wall to Bakery Corridor opens early, fall back into Kitchen Service and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
