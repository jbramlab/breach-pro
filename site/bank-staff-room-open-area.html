Staff Room / Open Area

You plus four randoms
AttackSolo1FDraft · Y11S3

Bring your own breach or your own anti-denial.

The plan

  1. Ace or Maverick opens the Staff Room wall to Printer even with no help.
  2. Drone Main Stairs for your randoms and call what you see.

Operators · first pick is you

  • ACAce
  • Maverick
  • Thatcher
  • Kali

Common mistake

Breaching without droning, so the denier just stays alive.

  • Kali

DefenceSolo1FDraft · Y11S3

Deny the Staff Room wall to Printer yourself and play from Open Area.

The plan

  1. Put your denial on the Staff Room wall to Printer so it survives even if your randoms do nothing.
  2. Reinforce the Staff Room wall to Printer before anything else.
  3. Call "Main Stairs" the moment you see a drone there.

  • Mute
  • Bandit
  • Mira

AttackOperator setup1F

Where each attacker goes on Staff Room / Open Area

  1. TM

    ThermiteHard breach · Printer

    Breach the Staff Room wall to Printer from Printer. Wait for Thatcher or Kali before you plant the charge.

  2. HI

    HibanaHard breach · Printer

    Backup breach on the Staff Room wall to Printer. Fire a pellet line low so you can shoot through before it opens fully.

  3. AC

    AceHard breach · Printer

    Throw both S.E.L.M.A.s on the Staff Room wall to Printer. If one gets shocked, the second still opens it.

  4. TH

    ThatcherAnti-gadget · Printer

    EMP the Staff Room wall to Printer right before the breach goes on. It wipes Kaid claws and Bandit batteries.

  5. KA

    KaliAnti-gadget · Main Stairs

    Lance the denial on the Staff Room wall to Printer from outside, and save a shot for the Mira in Open Area.

  6. AS

    AshEntry · Main Stairs

    Take Main Stairs first and clear the doorway into Open Area for the plant.

  7. TW

    TwitchGadget clear · Open Space Hallway

    Drive your shock drone into Open Area and zap the ADS, mirror and cams before the push.

  8. NO

    NomadFlank watch · Lobby Stairs

    Airjabs at Lobby Stairs so no roamer hits your backs on the plant.

DefenceOperator setup1F

Where each defender goes on Staff Room / Open Area

  1. KD

    KaidWall denial · Staff Room

    Claw the Staff Room wall to Printer from inside Staff Room so the whole wall is live. Your second claw goes on the Archives / side room wall.

  2. BA

    BanditWall denial · Staff Room

    Sit on the Staff Room wall to Printer with a battery in hand. Drop it the moment you hear a breach charge go on, then step back.

  3. MU

    MuteAnti-drone · Open Area

    Jammer by the Main Stairs door into Open Area. It stops drones getting in and stops remote breaches on that side.

  4. MI

    MiraAnchor intel · Open Area

    Mirror the wall between Open Area and Staff Room, so you can watch the plant in Staff Room from Open Area without peeking.

  5. JA

    JägerAnti-grenade · Open Area

    ADS on the Open Space Hallway side of Open Area. It eats the grenades thrown in before a push.

  6. VA

    ValkyrieCameras · Main Stairs

    Cams in Main Stairs and Open Space Hallway, high up. Call every drone and push you see.

  7. SM

    SmokePlant denial · Open Area

    Anchor Open Area. Save at least two canisters for the defuser, not for the first push.

  8. VI

    VigilRoam · Lobby Stairs

    Roam Lobby Stairs and the rooms around it. Take one fight, then fall back to Open Area by 1:00.

Attack basics

  • Hard breach the Staff Room wall to Printer from Printer.
  • Take Main Stairs early and drone the site.
  • Clear Lobby Stairs so nobody flanks the plant.

If it goes wrong

If the Staff Room wall to Printer stays denied, push Main Stairs and Open Space Hallway together with smokes and plant in Open Area.

In game

Defence basics

  • Reinforce the Staff Room wall to Printer first. It is the main hard breach.
  • Reinforce the Archives / side room wall next.
  • Watch Main Stairs and Open Space Hallway. That is where the inside push comes from.

If it goes wrong

If the Staff Room wall to Printer opens early, fall back into Open Area and play the plant instead of trading at the hole.
