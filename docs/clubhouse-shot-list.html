Before you start

  • Custom game, no enemies. Load Clubhouse twice, once as defence and once as attack, so you see the right spawns and prep phase.
  • Name each file with its code, for example CC-D2.png. That tells me exactly where it goes.
  • Wide and level. Stand in the doorway, aim at chest height, and fit as much of the room in as you can.
  • Leave walls unbroken for the "where to reinforce" and "where to breach" shots, so the wall is clearly visible.
